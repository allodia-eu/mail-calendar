# 0.10.0 — 2026-09-22

## macos

Paste into: App Store Connect (macOS)

Written, not assembled: 27 changes do not fit this store's cap. Every one of them is in the appendix below.

**English**

```
New: Folders inside other folders are now drawn inside them, each with its own arrow, remembered the next time you open the app. A message that cannot be sent right away waits in the Outbox and goes out by itself when the connection returns; you can send it now, edit it or cancel it. A folder that looks empty says whether it really is empty or whether older mail is still on the server. Contacts from a Google account appear in the app, including your colleagues on a work account. Mail arriving while the app is open raises a notification on macOS saying who it is from, what it is about and how it begins; clicking one opens that message, and you can turn it off in Settings → Notifications. Double-click a message to open it in a window of its own, and reply or forward from there in a window of its own too. Share a photo, a document or a link from the app you are in, and a message opens with it already attached. You can resize a picture in a message by dragging one of its corners, and it keeps its proportions. Setting a server up by hand now offers its port and its connection security beside the server name, and mail software running on your own machine, such as Proton Mail Bridge, can be connected once you have seen and accepted the certificate it identifies itself with. Settings shows your subscription: what you are being charged, by whom and when it renews, and on Mac, iPhone and iPad you can cancel it, start it again or switch period where Allodia bills you directly.

Fixed: Accepting, declining or answering "maybe" to an invitation works on a Microsoft account, which also shows the folders filed inside other folders and the mail in them. Archiving, deleting or flagging a message applies reliably, instead of the row returning a moment later. A folder whose mail could not be downloaded tries again the next time you open it. The folder pane highlights the folder you are reading, and Calendar, Contacts and Settings stay at the bottom of it while your accounts and folders stay on screen. Replying to a plain-text message keeps its line breaks and quote levels. A picture arrives at the size you gave it. A provider asking the app to slow down is no longer reported as an outage. Signing in with Google works on macOS, signing in to your Allodia account no longer briefly reports that you are signed out, and connecting an account no longer asks for a name your provider already knows. Assistant access starts when you switch it on, and you can select more than one line at a time in Settings → Diagnostics.
```

**Nederlands**

```
Nieuw: mappen in andere mappen staan nu ook echt daarin, met een eigen pijltje, dat de volgende keer nog zo staat. Een bericht dat niet meteen verstuurd kan worden, wacht in Postvak UIT en gaat vanzelf weg zodra de verbinding terug is; je kunt het nu versturen, bewerken of annuleren. Een map die leeg lijkt, geeft aan of hij echt leeg is of dat er nog oudere e-mail op de server staat. Contacten uit een Google-account staan nu in de app, inclusief je collega's bij een werkaccount. E-mail die binnenkomt terwijl de app openstaat, geeft een melding op macOS: van wie het is, waar het over gaat en hoe het begint; klik erop om dat bericht te openen, of zet ze uit in Instellingen → Meldingen. Dubbelklik op een bericht om het in een eigen venster te openen, en beantwoord of stuur het daar door in eveneens een eigen venster. Deel een foto, document of link vanuit de app waar je al bent, en er opent een bericht met de bijlage er al in. Een afbeelding in een bericht maak je groter of kleiner door aan een hoek te slepen, met behoud van de verhoudingen. Een server handmatig instellen biedt nu de poort en de beveiliging naast de servernaam, en mailsoftware op je eigen machine, zoals Proton Mail Bridge, kun je koppelen zodra je het certificaat hebt gezien en geaccepteerd. Instellingen laat je abonnement zien: wat je betaalt, aan wie en wanneer het wordt verlengd, en op Mac, iPhone en iPad kun je een abonnement dat Allodia rechtstreeks factureert opzeggen, hervatten of van periode wisselen.

Opgelost: een uitnodiging accepteren, weigeren of met "misschien" beantwoorden werkt nu op een Microsoft-account, dat ook de mappen in andere mappen laat zien en de e-mail erin. Archiveren, verwijderen of markeren werkt betrouwbaar, in plaats van dat de regel even later terugkomt. Een map waarvan de e-mail niet kon worden opgehaald, probeert het opnieuw wanneer je hem weer opent. Het mappenpaneel markeert de map die je leest, en Agenda, Contacten en Instellingen blijven onderin staan terwijl je accounts en mappen in beeld blijven. Antwoorden op een bericht in platte tekst behoudt de regeleindes en citaatniveaus. Een afbeelding komt aan op het formaat dat je haar gaf. Een provider die de app vraagt het rustiger aan te doen, wordt niet langer als storing gemeld. Aanmelden met Google werkt op macOS, aanmelden bij je Allodia-account meldt niet langer even dat je bent afgemeld, en bij het koppelen van een account vraagt de app niet meer naar een naam die je provider al kent. Assistent-toegang start zodra je hem aanzet, en je kunt meerdere regels tegelijk selecteren in Instellingen → Diagnostiek.
```

**Deutsch**

```
Neu: Ordner in anderen Ordnern stehen jetzt auch darin, mit einem eigenen Pfeil, der beim nächsten Start noch so steht. Eine Nachricht, die nicht sofort gesendet werden kann, wartet im Postausgang und geht von selbst raus, sobald die Verbindung wieder da ist; Sie können sie jetzt senden, bearbeiten oder abbrechen. Ein Ordner, der leer aussieht, gibt an, ob er wirklich leer ist oder ob ältere Nachrichten noch auf dem Server liegen. Kontakte aus einem Google-Konto erscheinen in der App, bei einem Arbeitskonto auch Ihre Kolleginnen und Kollegen. E-Mails, die eintreffen, während die App geöffnet ist, erscheinen unter macOS als Mitteilung: von wem sie sind, worum es geht und wie sie beginnen; ein Klick öffnet die Nachricht, abschaltbar unter Einstellungen → Benachrichtigungen. Öffnen Sie eine Nachricht per Doppelklick in einem eigenen Fenster, und antworten oder leiten Sie von dort ebenfalls in einem eigenen Fenster weiter. Teilen Sie ein Foto, ein Dokument oder einen Link aus der App, in der Sie gerade sind, und eine Nachricht öffnet sich mit dem Anhang. Ein Bild in einer Nachricht ziehen Sie an einer Ecke größer oder kleiner, die Proportionen bleiben erhalten. Beim manuellen Einrichten stehen Port und Verbindungssicherheit neben dem Servernamen, und E-Mail-Software auf Ihrem eigenen Rechner, etwa Proton Mail Bridge, lässt sich verbinden, sobald Sie das Zertifikat gesehen und angenommen haben. Die Einstellungen zeigen Ihr Abonnement: was Ihnen berechnet wird, von wem und wann es sich verlängert, und auf Mac, iPhone und iPad können Sie ein Abonnement, das Allodia direkt abrechnet, kündigen, fortsetzen oder den Zeitraum wechseln.

Behoben: Eine Einladung annehmen, ablehnen oder mit „vielleicht“ beantworten funktioniert jetzt bei einem Microsoft-Konto, das auch die Ordner in anderen Ordnern und die E-Mails darin zeigt. Archivieren, Löschen und Markieren werden zuverlässig übernommen, statt dass die Zeile kurz darauf zurückkehrt. Ein Ordner, dessen Nachrichten sich nicht laden ließen, versucht es beim nächsten Öffnen erneut. Der Ordnerbereich hebt den Ordner hervor, den Sie lesen, und Kalender, Kontakte und Einstellungen bleiben unten darin, während Ihre Konten und Ordner sichtbar bleiben. Die Antwort auf eine Nachricht in reinem Text behält Zeilenumbrüche und Zitatebenen. Ein Bild kommt in der Größe an, die Sie ihm gegeben haben. Ein Anbieter, der die App um Langsamkeit bittet, wird nicht mehr als Störung gemeldet. Die Anmeldung mit Google funktioniert unter macOS, die Anmeldung bei Ihrem Allodia-Konto meldet nicht mehr kurz, dass Sie abgemeldet sind, und beim Verbinden eines Kontos fragt die App nicht mehr nach einem Namen, den Ihr Anbieter bereits kennt. Der Assistenzzugriff startet, sobald Sie ihn einschalten, und unter Einstellungen → Diagnose lassen sich mehrere Zeilen auf einmal auswählen.
```

## Changes in this release

Engineering commentary, carried over from each fragment. Never pasted into a store.

### A folder inside a folder is drawn inside it — `a-folder-inside-a-folder-is-drawn-inside-it` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, minor)

> The pane drew every folder at one level, however the server nested them. That was legible only
> because two of the four transports named a folder by its whole path; now that every adapter
> reports the folder's own name and its parent, a flat list would put two rows called `2024` in
> one pane with nothing saying which Archive each is in. Each folder holding folders now carries
> its own disclosure control, remembered per account and folder across launches, and a folder
> nobody has touched shows what is inside it, so nothing that was on screen before goes missing.
> A role-bearing folder stays at the top whatever the server says, which is what keeps Sent and
> Trash out of Gmail's `[Gmail]` container.

### A message that cannot be sent waits in the Outbox — `a-message-that-cannot-be-sent-waits-in-the-outbox` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, minor)

> A send whose failure was worth retrying used to be recorded as failed and forgotten: the hint
> cleared after two and a half seconds, nothing retried the message, and no copy of it survived
> anywhere, because nothing could read the durable outbox back. The engine now keeps a queue that
> can be listed, retried and withdrawn, and the app shows it as one Outbox row above the accounts,
> present only while something is in it. The queue drains when the device comes back online, at
> the end of every sync pass, and when the user asks; reconnecting also clears each message's
> backoff, since the outage it was waiting out has just ended. Every client ships the row, the
> list and the three actions.

### An empty folder says why it is empty — `an-empty-folder-says-why-it-is-empty` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, minor)

> The badge beside a folder counts what the server holds, over all time, while the list can only

> The grid's height follows its viewport, and GTK reports a new viewport from inside the viewport's
> own size allocation, where neither a height nor a scroll offset asked for there reaches the
> screen. Both are now asked for from an idle, after the frame. That leaves one pass where the
> scrolled window measures the old day against the new size, so the offset is held as the minute at
> the middle of the viewport and put back once the geometry lands, rather than followed through a

> Both windows were built `transient_for` the mailbox, which is how GTK stacks a child above its

> composer window in front and the mailbox could not be clicked back over one. Neither window has a

> Settings included.

### No "your name" step where the provider already knows the name — `no-name-step-when-the-provider-knows-it` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, patch)

> The step was raised after every add, prefilled from `suggested_sender_name`, so an account

> already chosen elsewhere and a button to confirm it. Every add route now asks the core

### Paste a picture into a message, and into a signature — `paste-a-picture-into-a-message-or-signature` (linux, patch)

> WebKitGTK's paste `DataTransfer` answers `getData` for the text flavours and nothing else:
> `types`, `items` and `files` come back empty whatever is on the clipboard, and
> `getData("image/png")` is the empty string. So the shared bundle's paste handler, which every
> other host feeds a picture through, saw one here and inserted nothing, while a drop of the same
> file worked. The page cannot rescue it either: letting WebKit's own paste run yields
> `<img src="blob:…">`, and the composer's `connect-src 'none'` blocks `fetch` and
> `XMLHttpRequest` on that blob, leaving only a canvas re-encode that throws the original bytes
> away. The host now reads the clipboard itself and feeds the picture to the same
> `insertComposerImage` seam a dropped one arrives by, with the bytes sniffed against the core's
> own closed raster set and size cap, reached through a byte-shaped twin of the call a dropped file
> makes. Both routes into a paste are covered, the chord and the context menu's own Paste, and a
> paste carrying no picture is left to the page untouched, so pasting text is unchanged and so is
> the plain-text paste Ctrl+Shift+V asks for. Two traps cost a round of testing each and are now
> asserted: the chord controller has to sit on an ancestor, because WebKitGTK's own key controller
> is on the view and one added there is behind it; and a file manager's Copy puts no `image/*` on
> the clipboard at all, only `text/uri-list` and the portal's transfer types, so a copied picture
> arrives as the same `GdkFileList` a drop carries. A pasted picture is shown in the message
> without being asked about, unlike a dropped one: a paste is aimed at the caret and has already
> said what it is for. The Settings signature editor is the same bundle against the same toolkit, so
> it takes a pasted picture too; the clipboard is read once, in one place, and each editor is handed
> the bytes, because a signature's size rule is its own and far tighter than a message body's.
> The trail all of this leaves is `debug`: a paste that works is not a support event, and the
> refusals that matter are already logged by the core.

### Signing in no longer briefly reports that you are signed out — `signing-in-no-longer-reports-you-are-signed-out` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, patch)

> Storing a new grant drops the access token held for the process, because the service refuses one
> minted from the grant it supersedes. Every subsystem then asks for a token at the same instant:
> the account list, the subscription card and the purchase pass. Each read the same stored refresh
> token and each presented it, and the service rotates, so all but one were replays and were
> answered `invalid_grant`. That refusal is evidence a grant is dead, so the health flipped to
> signed out and the subscription card drew the failure, until the winning refresh flipped it back
> a few milliseconds later. Measured on a device, the two refreshes were 1 ms apart and the wrong
> answer was on screen for 101 ms. `allodia_access_token` now mints under a gate, which
> `mailcal-account` has held per account since two background passes rotated one mail grant twice.
> ⚠️ **The re-check inside the gate is the fix, not the lock.** A caller that merely waited its turn
> would present the token the winner had already spent, which is the same replay one round trip
> later; the regression test counts refreshes rather than errors, so a lock without the re-check
> fails it eight times over. What is still open: this shares state per core, and Android can build a
> second core, which `CredentialOrigin` solves for mail accounts and nothing yet solves here.

### Signing in with Google on macOS — `signing-in-with-google-on-macos` (macos, patch)

> Google's Desktop client type redirects to `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/`, so the macOS client binds a
> listener on loopback for the length of one sign-in. The App Sandbox refuses that bind without
> `com.apple.security.network.server`, which the entitlements did not grant, so the flow ended at
> `EPERM` before the browser ever opened and the setup sheet showed the raw errno. The sandbox
> applies to the Mac App Store build alone, so the notarized `.dmg` from the same commit signed in
> correctly and nothing in a build, a test or an upload could see it. The grant is the sandbox's and
> the reach is still the listener's own: it names `127.0.0.1` as its required local endpoint and is
> dropped when the redirect lands. The browser hop also writes to the diagnostic log now, per stage
> and for all four sign-in routes, so a sign-in that ends early says where.
