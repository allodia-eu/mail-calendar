## macos

**English**

```
The calendar now scrolls and zooms smoothly, and its events are tinted in their calendar's colour, show long titles in full where there is room, and mark repeating events.

A message you are writing beside the mailbox can now be moved into a window of its own, with everything you have written so far.

New Mail and the mail actions now share one bar that fits any window width and includes Move, and Delete always moves mail to Trash first, deleting for good only from Trash after asking.

Questions about a missing Sent copy, an undelivered invitation reply or a new time zone now wait as notices instead of interrupting, and each account’s page in Settings says whether its undelivered replies are emailed.

Anything that needs your attention now shows one notice at a time, in the sidebar on Mac and iPad and under the bar on iPhone, with the rest in one Issues list, each with its own button.

In Settings, an account's name now sits at the top of its page, and its mail settings show that a change is working until it is done.

Switching what an account is used for, linking it or removing it in Settings now shows that it is working until it is done.

In Settings, saving an account's servers now shows that it is working, says which field still needs filling in, and keeps what you typed if the servers are refused.

In Settings, switching a shared mailbox or adding one now shows that it is working, and an address that wasn't found stays to be corrected.

In Settings, an account's Sign-in section is where a refused password is entered again, and it shows that a new password is being tried until the server answers.

If your Allodia sign-in has ended and a paid feature will stop within a week, the notice now says so in the warning colour.

A calendar can now be written to as soon as its account reconnects, without waiting for a sync.

The formatting toolbar above a message you are writing is plainer, with icons for lists, and turns dark in a dark appearance; the message itself stays white, as its recipient will see it.

After you sign in again to give a missing permission, your calendar now connects straight away instead of at the next launch.

In the folder pane each account's name is now in bold, with its folders lined up beneath it and the chevrons at the start of each row.

Saving the name your mail is sent under no longer pauses the app while the server answers.

When Try again on a notice still cannot reach the server, the notice now says so.

The form for a calendar or address book on a server of its own now shows that Connect or Skip is working until it is done, on every platform.
```

## Changes in this release

Engineering commentary, carried over from each fragment. Never pasted into a store.

### The calendar grid scrolls and zooms smoothly, and events are drawn as Apple's Calendar draws them — `calendar-grid-smooth-and-tinted` (macos, ios, minor)

> The grid pulled every visible week across the FFI several times per frame, created date
> formatters per frame, re-ran the whole calendar screen (toolbar included) on every frame of
> motion, and built a SwiftUI view per event. Pages are now held per calendar version, the grid's
> position lives in its own observable object, and each week is one drawn canvas with an
> accessibility layer placed by the same geometry. The tinted block, the wrapped title and the
> corner glyphs are a product rule for every platform (docs/calendar.md §1, §4); the core serves
> the colours to all clients, and Windows, Android and Linux follow.
