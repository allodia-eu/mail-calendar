# 0.3.0 — 2026-08-03

## macos

Paste into: App Store Connect (macOS)

**English**

```
See who is coming to a meeting: who called it, and who has accepted or declined.

Answer a meeting invitation from the email itself, as the address it was sent to.

Contacts and address suggestions are now on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Now in seven languages — pick yours in Settings; it sets dates and weekday names too.

An expired JMAP sign-in is renewed in place — the account no longer has to be removed.

Let an AI assistant on your Mac use your mail — local, and off until you turn it on.

Search returns newest first, across every folder and account except the Trash.

An invitation email now shows a card: who, when, where, and your calendar that day.

Signatures: write one once, and pick per account which one starts a message.

Archiving or deleting opens the next message; on Mac and Windows the calendar scrolls.

The calendar opens framed on today instead of scrolled past everything in it.

Fixed: JMAP accounts such as Fastmail opened their messages blank.

The Mac remembers how wide you made the panes, and keeps them that way.

Meetings you organised no longer look like invitations you haven’t answered.

Fixed: replying to a plain-text message no longer loses the original.

Unread mail shows subject and sender in bold, so a full mailbox is scannable again.
```

**Nederlands**

```
Zie wie er bij een afspraak komt: wie hem belegde, en wie accepteerde of afwees.

Beantwoord een uitnodiging vanuit de e-mail zelf, met het adres waaraan hij is gestuurd.

Contacten en adressuggesties zijn er nu ook op Mac, iPhone en iPad.

Nu in zeven talen — kies de jouwe bij Instellingen; dat bepaalt ook datums en dagnamen.

Een verlopen JMAP-aanmelding vernieuw je ter plekke — het account hoeft er niet uit.

Laat een AI-assistent op je Mac je e-mail gebruiken — lokaal, en uit tot je hem aanzet.

Zoeken geeft nieuwste eerst, in elke map en elk account behalve de prullenbak.

Een uitnodiging toont nu een kaart: wie, wanneer, waar, en je agenda die dag.

Handtekeningen: schrijf er één keer één en kies per account welke een bericht opent.

Archiveren of verwijderen opent het volgende bericht; op Mac en Windows scrolt de agenda.

De agenda opent met vandaag in beeld, in plaats van voorbij alles gescrold.

Opgelost: bij JMAP-accounts zoals Fastmail bleven berichten leeg.

De Mac onthoudt hoe breed je de vensters maakt, en houdt ze zo.

Afspraken die je zelf belegde lijken niet langer op onbeantwoorde uitnodigingen.

Opgelost: bij antwoorden op een bericht zonder opmaak verdwijnt het origineel niet meer.

Ongelezen post toont onderwerp én afzender vet, zodat een vol postvak leesbaar blijft.
```

**Deutsch**

```
Sehen Sie, wer zu einem Termin kommt: wer ihn einberief und wer zu- oder absagte.

Beantworten Sie eine Einladung direkt in der E-Mail — mit der Adresse, an die sie ging.

Kontakte und Adressvorschläge gibt es jetzt auch auf Mac, iPhone und iPad.

Jetzt in sieben Sprachen — wählbar unter Einstellungen, samt Datums- und Tagesnamen.

Eine abgelaufene JMAP-Anmeldung wird direkt erneuert — kein Entfernen des Kontos mehr.

Ein KI-Assistent auf Ihrem Mac kann Ihre E-Mail nutzen — lokal, standardmäßig aus.

Die Suche liefert Neuestes zuerst — über alle Ordner und Konten außer dem Papierkorb.

Eine Einladung zeigt jetzt eine Karte: wer, wann, wo — und Ihr Kalender an dem Tag.

Signaturen: einmal schreiben, pro Konto wählen, welche eine Nachricht eröffnet.

Archivieren oder Löschen öffnet die nächste Nachricht; auf Mac und Windows scrollt der Kalender.

Der Kalender öffnet auf heute, statt an allem vorbeigescrollt zu sein.

Behoben: Bei JMAP-Konten wie Fastmail blieben Nachrichten leer.

Der Mac merkt sich, wie breit Sie die Bereiche gezogen haben, und behält das bei.

Selbst einberufene Termine sehen nicht mehr wie unbeantwortete Einladungen aus.

Behoben: Beim Antworten auf eine Nur-Text-Nachricht geht das Original nicht mehr verloren.

Ungelesene Post zeigt Betreff und Absender fett — ein volles Postfach bleibt lesbar.
```

**Français**

```
Voyez qui vient à une réunion : qui l’a convoquée, et qui a accepté ou refusé.

Répondez à une invitation depuis l’e-mail même, avec l’adresse qui a été invitée.

Les contacts et les suggestions d’adresses arrivent sur Mac, iPhone et iPad.

Désormais en sept langues — à choisir dans les Réglages, dates et jours compris.

Une connexion JMAP expirée se renouvelle sur place — plus besoin de retirer le compte.

Un assistant IA sur votre Mac peut utiliser votre courrier — local, désactivé par défaut.

La recherche affiche le plus récent d’abord, dans tous les dossiers sauf la corbeille.

Une invitation affiche une fiche : qui, quand, où, et votre agenda ce jour-là.

Signatures : rédigez-en une, puis choisissez par compte celle qui ouvre un message.

Archiver ou supprimer ouvre le message suivant ; sur Mac et Windows, l’agenda défile.

L’agenda s’ouvre cadré sur aujourd’hui, au lieu d’être défilé au-delà de tout.

Corrigé : les comptes JMAP comme Fastmail ouvraient des messages vides.

Le Mac retient la largeur que vous donnez aux volets, et la conserve.

Les réunions que vous organisez ne ressemblent plus à des invitations sans réponse.

Corrigé : répondre à un message en texte brut ne perd plus l’original.

Le courrier non lu affiche objet et expéditeur en gras : une boîte pleine reste lisible.
```

**Español**

```
Mira quién asiste a una reunión: quién la convocó y quién aceptó o rechazó.

Responde a una invitación desde el propio correo, con la dirección a la que se envió.

Los contactos y las sugerencias de direcciones llegan a Mac, iPhone y iPad.

El calendario se abre centrado en hoy, no desplazado más allá de todo.

Corregido: las cuentas JMAP como Fastmail abrían mensajes en blanco.

El Mac recuerda el ancho que das a los paneles y lo conserva.

Las reuniones que organizas ya no parecen invitaciones sin responder.

Corregido: responder a un mensaje sin formato ya no pierde el original.

El correo sin leer muestra asunto y remitente en negrita: un buzón lleno se lee mejor.

**Italiano**

```

Archiviare o eliminare apre il messaggio successivo; su Mac e Windows il calendario scorre.

Il calendario si apre inquadrato su oggi, non scorso oltre tutto.

Risolto: gli account JMAP come Fastmail aprivano messaggi vuoti.

Il Mac ricorda quanto hai allargato i riquadri e li lascia così.

Le riunioni che organizzi non sembrano più inviti senza risposta.

Risolto: rispondere a un messaggio di solo testo non perde più l’originale.

### JMAP sign-ins stop expiring — and one that has can be renewed from the app — `jmap-sign-in-renewed-in-place` (macos, ios, windows, android, minor)

> **Migrated from the old single-file changelog.** This shipped as the `0.15.0` entry in #193, which
> landed on `main` while this branch was in review — under the scheme where a user-facing change
> bumped `/VERSION` itself. It is transcribed here 1:1, seven locales and engineering commentary
> intact, exactly like the nineteen before it. The version number it carried is deliberately **not**
> preserved: `0.15.0` reached no store, and under this scheme the next release computes its own
> number from the `Bump:` lines.
>
> **A capability a user can see, so a minor bump** ([`versioning.md`](../../versioning.md)). **macOS,
> iOS/iPadOS, Windows and Android** — every platform that ships the JMAP sign-in — so rule 4 lets
> the note run unqualified. Linux has no account setup at all and is unaffected.
>
> **The reconnect button is half of this; the other half is why it was needed.** A production
> Fastmail account died with `invalid_grant — ratchet or client_id mismatch`, and the log said
> nothing else because a token refresh was logged at DEBUG. Two defects, both invisible on
> Microsoft and Google because those providers forgive them: an account's providers (JMAP has
> three — mail, calendar, contacts) could each post their **own** token refresh carrying the
> **same** refresh token, and Fastmail treats the second as a replay and revokes the grant; and
> the **cold background worker** registered no secure-store writer at all, so every rotation in a
> background pass was computed and dropped, leaving a stored token the server had moved past.
> Fixed by single-flighting the refresh, by making the stores **constructor parameters** of the
> headless worker (forgetting them is now a compile error), and by logging every refresh at INFO.
> The user-facing note covers the reconnect, because "we stopped destroying your sign-in" is not
> a sentence a release note should have to contain.
>
> **Verified on all four platforms against a real dead Fastmail grant** (2026-08-01), covering
> both ways one dies — the ratchet revoking it, and a manual revoke in Fastmail's UI. Each took ~6–14 s:
> the prompt offered the button, the authorization opened with no discovery call, the rotated token
> reached the platform keystore, the prompt retracted, and the account resumed with a catch-up
> rather than re-downloading. The same boots proved the new refresh logging on real devices —
> Google kept its refresh token, Graph and JMAP both rotated theirs. **Windows** ran last (8.2 s,
> the token landing in the Credential Manager and surviving a cold relaunch), which is why this
> note goes to **all three stores** — the README cell is ✅ everywhere the JMAP sign-in ships, so
> rule 4 is satisfied.
>
> **This was a dead end, not a missing nicety.** An expired or revoked grant already raised the
> "your sign-in has expired" banner on every client, and for Microsoft and Google that banner has a
> button. A JMAP account got the *other* wording — "update it in Settings" — where nothing could
> actually update it: the only cure was to remove the account and add it back, losing the local
> store and re-downloading the mailbox. Fastmail is the account this was reported on.
>
> **The reconnect re-authorizes the account's own stored grant** — its discovered authorization
> endpoint, its registered client id, its scopes — rather than re-running discovery and dynamic
> registration, which would mint a second client on the user's provider account for every reconnect
> and orphan the first.
>
> **A JMAP account is not one kind of account**, which is why the button can't simply be shown for
> all of them: one connected by signing in has a flow to re-run, one holding a pasted password or
> API token has none and is still pointed at Settings. Only the account's own config knows which,
> so the core now reports the two families apart.

### The Mac remembers how wide you made the panes — `mac-remembers-pane-widths` (macos, patch)

> **One user-visible fix, so a patch bump** ([`versioning.md`](versioning.md)). **Mac only** — pure
> client work, no Rust and no FFI. Rule 4 scopes the note to the Mac line accordingly; Windows has
> persisted its pane width since `PaneLayoutStore`, and the phone and tablet layouts have no
> draggable divider to remember.
>
> **The widths were saved and restored correctly all along** — what threw them away was *leaving the
> mailbox*. AppKit restores an autosaved split layout when the split view is **set up** and never
> again, and the shell put its destination `switch` inside the split, so the panes were torn down
> and rebuilt on every Mail ⇄ Calendar ⇄ Contacts move. The rebuilt layout took the extremes — the
> sidebar to its `maxWidth`, the list to its `minWidth` — and was then saved over the good one.
>
> **Nesting the splits was necessary but not sufficient**, which is the part worth writing down.
> Giving each destination its own inner split fixed the list and left the sidebar still snapping,
> because SwiftUI rebuilds a `_ConditionalContent` subtree whenever the branch changes — **even
> between two branches of identical shape** (Mail → Contacts → Mail, both nested splits, still
> snapped). The second half is a container the branch change happens *inside*, so the outer split
> never sees a pane go away.
>
> **No new preference and no new persistence layer.** The widths already lived in AppKit's autosave;
> this stops discarding them. The autosave names are new (`…SidebarV3` / `…MailV3` /
> `…ContactsV3`) because the old key described a three-pane split that no longer exists — so an
> existing install starts from the default widths once, then keeps whatever you drag.
