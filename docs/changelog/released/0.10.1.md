# 0.10.1 — 2026-09-27

## macos

Paste into: App Store Connect (macOS)

**English**

```
The first sync of an IMAP account now downloads its messages several times faster, and carries on through a dropped connection.

When a mail server temporarily turns a sign-in away because of a connection limit, the app no longer says your password is wrong or asks you to sign in again.

The first sync of a JMAP account downloads its messages faster on servers that allow many to be fetched at once.

Mail downloaded to your iPhone, iPad or Mac is no longer included in iCloud or Time Machine backups, so it no longer takes up your iCloud storage; after a restore it is downloaded again from your mail server.

The subscription screen now links the Terms of Use and the privacy policy beside the Subscribe buttons.

New mail on a newly added IMAP account now appears as it arrives, instead of only after the app is restarted.

IMAP accounts now use at most five connections across all their folders, so a server that limits connections no longer makes your password look wrong; push now watches up to four folders per account.
```

**Nederlands**

```
De eerste synchronisatie van een IMAP-account downloadt berichten nu een stuk sneller en gaat door na een verbroken verbinding.

Als een mailserver een aanmelding tijdelijk weigert vanwege een limiet op verbindingen, zegt de app niet langer dat je wachtwoord onjuist is en vraagt hij niet opnieuw om je aan te melden.

De eerste synchronisatie van een JMAP-account downloadt berichten sneller op servers die er veel tegelijk laten ophalen.

Mail die naar uw iPhone, iPad of Mac is gedownload, wordt niet meer meegenomen in iCloud- of Time Machine-reservekopieën en neemt dus geen iCloud-opslag meer in; na een herstel wordt deze opnieuw van uw mailserver gedownload.

Het abonnementsscherm linkt nu naar de gebruiksvoorwaarden en het privacybeleid, naast de knoppen om een abonnement te nemen.

Nieuwe e-mail op een zojuist toegevoegd IMAP-account verschijnt nu zodra deze binnenkomt, in plaats van pas na het herstarten van de app.

IMAP-accounts gebruiken nu hoogstens vijf verbindingen voor al hun mappen, zodat een server die verbindingen beperkt uw wachtwoord niet langer onjuist laat lijken; push volgt nu tot vier mappen per account.
```

**Deutsch**

```
Die erste Synchronisierung eines IMAP-Kontos lädt Nachrichten jetzt um ein Vielfaches schneller herunter und setzt nach einer unterbrochenen Verbindung fort.

Wenn ein Mailserver eine Anmeldung wegen einer Verbindungsgrenze vorübergehend ablehnt, meldet die App nicht mehr, Ihr Passwort sei falsch, und fordert Sie nicht erneut zur Anmeldung auf.

Die erste Synchronisierung eines JMAP-Kontos lädt Nachrichten schneller herunter, wenn der Server viele auf einmal ausliefert.

Auf Ihr iPhone, iPad oder Ihren Mac geladene E-Mails werden nicht mehr in iCloud- oder Time Machine-Backups aufgenommen und belegen daher keinen iCloud-Speicher mehr; nach einer Wiederherstellung werden sie erneut von Ihrem Mailserver geladen.

Der Abo-Bereich verlinkt jetzt die Nutzungsbedingungen und die Datenschutzerklärung neben den Schaltflächen zum Abonnieren.

Neue E-Mails in einem soeben hinzugefügten IMAP-Konto erscheinen jetzt beim Eintreffen, statt erst nach einem Neustart der App.

IMAP-Konten verwenden jetzt höchstens fünf Verbindungen für alle ihre Ordner, sodass ein Server, der Verbindungen begrenzt, Ihr Passwort nicht mehr falsch erscheinen lässt; Push überwacht jetzt bis zu vier Ordner pro Konto.
```

**Français**

```
La première synchronisation d'un compte IMAP télécharge désormais les messages bien plus rapidement et se poursuit après une coupure de connexion.

Lorsqu'un serveur de messagerie refuse temporairement une connexion en raison d'une limite de connexions, l'app n'indique plus que votre mot de passe est incorrect et ne vous demande plus de vous reconnecter.

La première synchronisation d'un compte JMAP télécharge les messages plus rapidement sur les serveurs qui en fournissent plusieurs à la fois.

Les e-mails téléchargés sur votre iPhone, iPad ou Mac ne sont plus inclus dans les sauvegardes iCloud ou Time Machine et n'occupent donc plus votre stockage iCloud ; après une restauration, ils sont à nouveau téléchargés depuis votre serveur de messagerie.

L’écran d’abonnement propose désormais les conditions d’utilisation et la politique de confidentialité à côté des boutons d’abonnement.

Les nouveaux e-mails d'un compte IMAP que vous venez d'ajouter apparaissent désormais dès leur arrivée, et non plus seulement après le redémarrage de l'application.

Les comptes IMAP utilisent désormais au plus cinq connexions pour tous leurs dossiers, de sorte qu'un serveur qui limite les connexions ne fait plus paraître votre mot de passe incorrect ; le push surveille désormais jusqu'à quatre dossiers par compte.
```

**Español**

```
La primera sincronización de una cuenta IMAP descarga ahora los mensajes mucho más rápido y continúa tras una conexión interrumpida.

Cuando un servidor de correo rechaza temporalmente un inicio de sesión por un límite de conexiones, la aplicación ya no indica que la contraseña es incorrecta ni le pide que vuelva a iniciar sesión.

El correo descargado en tu iPhone, iPad o Mac ya no se incluye en las copias de seguridad de iCloud ni de Time Machine, por lo que deja de ocupar tu almacenamiento de iCloud; tras una restauración se vuelve a descargar desde tu servidor de correo.

La pantalla de suscripción enlaza ahora los términos de uso y la política de privacidad junto a los botones para suscribirse.

El correo nuevo de una cuenta IMAP recién añadida aparece ahora en cuanto llega, en lugar de solo después de reiniciar la aplicación.

Las cuentas IMAP usan ahora como máximo cinco conexiones para todas sus carpetas, así que un servidor que limita las conexiones ya no hace que tu contraseña parezca incorrecta; el push vigila ahora hasta cuatro carpetas por cuenta.
```

**Italiano**

Gli account IMAP ora usano al massimo cinque connessioni per tutte le loro cartelle, quindi un server che limita le connessioni non fa più sembrare errata la tua password; il push ora controlla fino a quattro cartelle per account.
```

**Português**

```
A primeira sincronização de uma conta IMAP descarrega agora as mensagens muito mais depressa e continua após uma ligação interrompida.

Quando um servidor de correio recusa temporariamente um início de sessão devido a um limite de ligações, a aplicação já não indica que a palavra-passe está incorreta nem pede para iniciar sessão novamente.

As contas IMAP usam agora no máximo cinco ligações para todas as suas pastas, pelo que um servidor que limita as ligações já não faz parecer errada a sua palavra-passe; o push vigia agora até quatro pastas por conta.

## Changes in this release

Engineering commentary, carried over from each fragment. Never pasted into a store.

### Download an IMAP account's messages in batches, over several connections — `download-imap-mail-in-batches` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, minor)

> The body warm after an IMAP sync asked for one message at a time, each paying two round
> trips, so a first sync of a real mailbox took minutes on any connection. It now asks for
> batches of messages in one request per folder, several at once over the account's
> connections, and keeps going through dropped connections and a server that allows fewer
> connections than we asked for.

### A mail server's connection limit is no longer reported as a wrong password — `a-connection-limit-is-not-a-wrong-password` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, patch)

> The engine read every refused IMAP `LOGIN` and every refused SMTP `AUTH` as a bad credential.
> Yahoo refuses the sixth session opened within a few seconds with `NO [LIMIT]`, and the app
> opens one session per synced folder, so a Yahoo account added with a correct app password was
> told its password was wrong, and an existing account hitting the limit at launch raised the
> reconnect prompt. The engine now classifies `[LIMIT]` at `LOGIN` and a 4xx at `AUTH` as a rate
> limit, so the first login no longer becomes `SigninRejected`. Adding a Yahoo account still fails
> until one connection serves several folders; this change only stops the failure being blamed on
> the password.

### A quieter folder pane on iPad and iPhone — `a-quieter-folder-pane-on-ipad-and-iphone` (ios, patch)

> The iPad sidebar drew the app's name over the accounts, beside the status bar's copy of it; the
> title is still set for the OS and now hidden, as on macOS. The pinned destinations were a bar in
> the grouped background over the scrolling tree, a lighter slab on the iPad's sidebar surface and
> short of the bottom edge, so rows showed beneath it; on iOS they are now stacked under the tree
> with no fill of their own.

### Back up Android settings without the mail — `back-up-android-settings-without-the-mail` (android, patch)

> Auto Backup takes nothing from an app whose data passes 25 MB, and the mail store did so with any
> real mailbox. `data_extraction_rules.xml` now leaves the store out of cloud backup and device
> transfer, so the rest of the app's data fits again.

### Download a JMAP account's messages in batches where the server offers it — `download-jmap-mail-in-batches` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, patch)

> On a JMAP server that offers `Blob/get` (RFC 9404), such as Stalwart and Thundermail, the
> body warm now reads 25 messages per request instead of one. Stalwart also limits an account to
> about 1,000 requests a minute, which one request per message reached on any sizeable mailbox.
> When a server does refuse for now, the warm waits (its stated time, or a minute where it names
> none) and carries on in the same pass, rather than leaving those messages for the next sync.

### Keep the Linux calendar's day names on screen, and draw the hours it scrolls to — `draw-the-linux-calendar-hours-it-scrolls-to` (linux, patch)

> The grid paints only the events inside its viewport, and GTK moves a scrolled child without
> redrawing it, so the hours the calendar opened on stayed empty until something else caused a
> redraw. The day names and the all-day banner were painted inside the scrolled day, so opening on
> the current hour scrolled them out of sight; they are now a surface of their own above it.

### Keep downloaded mail out of backups — `keep-downloaded-mail-out-of-backups` (macos, ios, patch)

> The mail store (the SQLite database, its WAL and shared-memory files and the blob directory
> holding message sources) is flagged `isExcludedFromBackup`, so a large mailbox no longer fills
> the user's iCloud storage through the device backup. Preferences and signatures stay in it.

### Link the Terms of Use and the privacy policy beside a subscription — `link-the-terms-and-privacy-policy-beside-a-subscription` (macos, ios, patch)

> Apple's guideline 3.1.2 asks a screen selling an auto-renewable subscription to link the Terms
> of Use and the privacy policy beside the purchase, and the Apple subscription screen linked
> neither. The terms are Apple's standard EULA, the one the App Store product page links; see
> `allodia_license/purchasing.md`. The other clients bill through Google or Allodia, so Apple's
> EULA does not apply there and they are unchanged.

### Linux: an icon beside each Settings category, and a real one on an empty folder — `linux-settings-category-icons` (linux, patch)

> The Settings sidebar was the one surface drawing no icons at all; it now carries the same
> meanings the other platforms draw, from Adwaita where it has them and from GNOME's
> icon-development-kit where it does not. The empty-folder page named an icon Adwaita 50 does not
> ship, so it drew GTK's broken-image placeholder; it now shows the inbox tray, as Apple does.

### Push a new IMAP account's mail from its first sync — `push-a-new-imap-account-from-its-first-sync` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, patch)

> A newly added IMAP account started its push watches before its first sync had stored any
> folders, so it watched none, and new mail waited for a manual refresh until the next launch.
> The watches now start once the first sync has the account's mail on screen.

### Share an IMAP account's connections across its folders — `share-an-imap-accounts-connections-across-its-folders` (macos, ios, windows, android, linux, patch)

> An IMAP account opened one connection per synced folder and one per watched folder, all held
> for the life of the app, and redialled all of them at once after a network change. A server
> with a per-user connection limit refuses the excess at login, which the app read as a wrong
> password. The account now holds at most five, shared by every folder, and push watches up to
> four folders so one connection is always left to sync.
