# Saved replies stay in their conversation

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> A reply or forward saved as a draft before it was sent was stored without the headers that
> place it in its conversation, so it reopened, and went out, as a new message; a reply started
> from the message list quoted nothing; an assistant's reply draft opened as a new message. Every
> composer now opens through one call that joins it to its conversation and quotes the original.

**English**

```
A reply or forward saved as a draft now stays in its conversation, and a reply started from the message list quotes the original.
```
