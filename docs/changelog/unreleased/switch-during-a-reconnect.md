# A use switched during a reconnect stays switched

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> Switching an account's calendar or contacts on or off while the app was reconnecting it no
> longer comes undone until the next refresh: the account is connected again as it now is.

**English**

```
Switching an account’s calendar or contacts on or off while it reconnects now takes effect straight away.
```
