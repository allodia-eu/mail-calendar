# The composer spaces a message as it is sent

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> The editor's own margins above the signature and the quote, and under the quote's attribution,
> are gone: the renderer never sent them. A new message and a reply open with a real blank line
> above the signature, and between it and a quote, as Outlook does; a drafted reply keeps one.

**English**

```
The composer now shows your message spaced exactly as it is sent, with a blank line above your signature as in Outlook.
```
