# Sharing to the app on iPhone and iPad opens it

Platforms: ios
Bump: patch

> The Share Extension staged what was shared but could not bring the app forward, so the share
> sheet closed and the composer waited until the app was next opened. It now opens the app with the
> composer, and says where the share is waiting when the system will not.
