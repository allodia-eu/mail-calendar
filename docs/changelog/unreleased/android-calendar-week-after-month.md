# The Android calendar's week opens on its first day after the month

Platforms: android
Bump: patch

> Choosing a week, work week or day from the view menu while on the month seated the week on the
> 1st of the month, so July 2026 put a Wednesday under the first heading; the same after the month
> and then the agenda. And a calendar reopened on the month in the first days of a month whose first
> week began in the month before showed that earlier month. The pager now seats every week through
> the core's week start, and seeds the month with today.

**English**

```
Switching from the month view to the week view starts the week on its first day again, and the month view opens on the current month.
```
