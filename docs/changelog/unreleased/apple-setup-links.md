# Link a new account to another while setting it up on Mac, iPhone and iPad

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> When another account can fill what a new IMAP or calendar account lacks, setup offers it as a
> last, skippable step: a calendar or address book from another account, or for a calendar
> without mail, the account that sends its invitations. A suggested account starts picked, and
> "Add another account" sets one up and comes back to the step. Each account added is asked its
> name once setup closes, one after another, and an account without mail is not asked at all.

**English**

```
Setting up an IMAP or CalDAV account offers to use another account's calendar or contacts with it.
```
