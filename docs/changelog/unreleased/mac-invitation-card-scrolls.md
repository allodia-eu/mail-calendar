# A tall invitation card scrolls inside the Mac reading pane

Platforms: macos
Bump: patch

> The Mac reading column does not scroll as a whole, so an invitation card taller than the room left
> under the header ran past the bottom of the pane, Accept, Maybe and Decline with it, with nothing
> to scroll them into view. The card now draws at its own height while it fits, and scrolls inside
> itself once it would crowd the pane, sharing the height with the message below it.
