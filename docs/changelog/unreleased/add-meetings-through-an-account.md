# Add a Teams or Talk meeting through a connected account

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> An account gains a Video meetings use: a Microsoft work account makes Teams meetings, a setup
> choice that is on for a new account and asks Microsoft for `OnlineMeetings.ReadWrite`, and a
> Nextcloud account makes Talk meetings with its own app password, on unless switched off. Each adds a choice, named with the
> account's address, to every writable calendar's Video meeting row. The meeting is made before
> the event is written, and deleted again if the event could not be saved.

**English**

```
Add a Microsoft Teams or Nextcloud Talk meeting to an event on any calendar, through your Microsoft work account or your Nextcloud account.
```
