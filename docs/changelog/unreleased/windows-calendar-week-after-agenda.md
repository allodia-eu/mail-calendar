# The Windows calendar's week opens on its first day after the agenda or the month

Platforms: windows
Bump: patch

> A calendar last left on the agenda or the month seated the week, from the view menu or Today, on
> today rather than on the week's first day, so a Tuesday stood under the first heading.

**English**

```
Opening the week view after the agenda or month view starts the week on its first day again, not on today.
```

**Nederlands**

```
Wie na de agenda- of maandweergave de weekweergave opent, ziet de week weer beginnen op de eerste dag in plaats van op vandaag.
```

**Deutsch**

```
Wenn Sie nach der Agenda- oder Monatsansicht die Wochenansicht öffnen, beginnt die Woche wieder mit ihrem ersten Tag statt mit heute.
```

**Français**

```
Ouvrir la vue semaine après la vue agenda ou mois fait de nouveau commencer la semaine par son premier jour, et non par aujourd’hui.
```

**Español**

```
Abrir la vista de semana después de la agenda o del mes vuelve a empezar la semana por su primer día, no por hoy.
```

**Italiano**

```
Aprendo la vista settimana dopo l’agenda o il mese, la settimana torna a iniziare dal suo primo giorno e non da oggi.
```

**Português**

