# One notice at a time, in the sidebar on the Mac and the iPad and under the bar on iPhone

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> The banners that could stack above the list (offline, an expired sign-in, a missing permission,
> the Allodia sign-in, a refused folder change, a calendar or address book that did not connect)
> are now the core's notices: one drawn per screen, the rest behind one Issues list, each with its
> own button, ranked the same as on every other platform. Each account carries its own in the
> sidebar and at the top of its Settings page. A remedy that fails says so on the notice rather
> than in a separate line.

**English**

```
Anything that needs your attention now shows one notice at a time, in the sidebar on Mac and iPad and under the bar on iPhone, with the rest in one Issues list, each with its own button.
```
