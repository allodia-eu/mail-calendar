# Android draws the core's notices: one card per screen, and the account it is about

Platforms: android
Bump: minor

> What needs attention (a sign-in that stopped working, a calendar that did not connect, being
> offline) is now one card at the top of the screen, with the rest behind "N more issues", each
> with its own button; the account it is about says so in the folder list and on its page in
> Settings.

**English**

```
Anything that needs your attention now appears in one card at the top of the screen, with its own button, and the account it concerns says so in the folder list.
```
