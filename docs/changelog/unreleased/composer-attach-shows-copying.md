# Attach shows that chosen files are still on their way

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> The composer's Attach is a control the core gives a state: it runs from the moment files the
> person chose are on their way until they are on the message, which on iPhone and iPad covers a
> film from the photo library being converted after its picker has closed.

**English**

```
The attach button now shows that it is working while files you chose, such as a video from your photo library, are still being prepared.
```
