# A calendar can be written as soon as its account reconnects

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> After an account was reconnected, for instance when one of its uses was switched, its calendars
> stayed read-only until the next calendar sync, about a minute on a large calendar. They now
> follow the account's connection, as its Teams or Talk choice already did.

**English**

```
A calendar can now be written to as soon as its account reconnects, without waiting for a sync.
```
