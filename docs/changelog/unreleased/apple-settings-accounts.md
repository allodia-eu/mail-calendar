# Every account in Settings → Accounts on Mac, iPhone and iPad, each with a page of its own

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> Settings → Accounts drew one card per mail account from `sync_settings`. It now lists every
> account from the core's snapshot, a calendar or contacts account included, each opening a page:
> what it is used for, the accounts it is linked to with a suggested one named, signing a Microsoft
> or Google account in again in place, a password account's servers and password tried before they
> are saved, a new secret for a JMAP account whose pasted one stopped working, its mail settings,
> and a removal that names the accounts losing their link. The main window's sign-in prompts now
> sign an account in again in place rather than adding it a second time, removing the last account
> returns to setup, and an account without mail is named by its address in the prompts and in a
> contact's provenance.
