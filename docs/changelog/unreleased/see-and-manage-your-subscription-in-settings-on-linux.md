# See and change your subscription in Settings on Linux

Platforms: linux
Bump: minor

> The last desktop client to owe this screen, now drawn: Settings → Allodia account carries the
> subscription below the account box and only while somebody is signed in. What it draws is what
> the core answered, `actions` decides which buttons exist, and a store's subscription is read-only
> here, so its button opens the page that store sent. Flatpak has no commerce, so the one shop is
> Allodia's own checkout, and it leaves for the browser through the desktop portal like every other
> URI this client hands over, never a web view. All four writes are here for the same reason
> Windows's are, each only where `actions` permits, and both that change what somebody is charged
> ask first, as an alert dialog rather than Windows's inline question: the cancellation names the
> day access runs to, and its dismiss says "Keep subscription", because on that question the word
> "Cancel" is the thing being asked about. The switch quotes no price; the amount comes back from
> the switch itself and is said afterwards. The store-billed shape was read against a live account
> (2026-09-20): "Billed by Google Play", its own page offered, and none of the three writes drawn,
> which is the half `actions` refuses. The card asks again whenever the Settings window comes back
> to the front, because a checkout finishes in a browser with that window still open behind it, and
> without it somebody returns to a card still offering to sell what they have just bought.
> A subscription of Allodia's own was cancelled and restarted from this client in the same session,
> ending where it began. ⚠️ Starting a checkout and switching period are still unrun here.

```
Settings now shows your subscription: what you’re being charged and until when, and lets you subscribe, cancel, start again or move between monthly and yearly.
```
