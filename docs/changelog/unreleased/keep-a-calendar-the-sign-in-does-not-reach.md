# Keep a calendar the sign-in does not reach

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> Switching a password account to its provider's sign-in, where the provider's tokens do not reach
> its calendar and address book server, dropped both with nothing said. The switch now asks first
> and can keep them on the password as a linked account, and setup and the account's page can set
> up that server with its own password.

**English**

```
When signing in through your provider would cost your calendar and contacts, the app asks first and can keep them on your password as a separate, linked account.
```
