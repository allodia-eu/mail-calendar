# Link the Terms of Use and the privacy policy beside a subscription

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: patch

> Apple's guideline 3.1.2 asks a screen selling an auto-renewable subscription to link the Terms
> of Use and the privacy policy beside the purchase, and the Apple subscription screen linked
> neither. The terms are Apple's standard EULA, the one the App Store product page links; see
> `allodia_license/purchasing.md`. The other clients bill through Google or Allodia, so Apple's
> EULA does not apply there and they are unchanged.

**English**

```
The subscription screen now links the Terms of Use and the privacy policy beside the Subscribe buttons.
```
