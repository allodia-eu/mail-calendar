# Open a calendar shared with you

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> A store a JMAP, CalDAV or Microsoft 365 account's sign-in opens besides its own, such as a
> group's or a colleague's, is offered on the account's page with what it holds (on Microsoft, by
> its address, under the use that already opened shared mailboxes) and, once switched on, is an
> account of its own with its owner's mail and calendars and their rights: a group's mailbox and
> calendar are one switch.

**English**

```
Calendars shared with an account, such as a team's, can be switched on from the account's page together with the team's mailbox, and appear as an account of their own.
```

**Nederlands**

```
Agenda’s die met een account zijn gedeeld, zoals die van een team, zet je samen met de mailbox van het team aan op de pagina van het account; ze verschijnen als eigen account.
```

**Deutsch**

```
Kalender, die mit einem Konto geteilt sind, etwa der eines Teams, lassen sich zusammen mit dem Postfach des Teams auf der Seite des Kontos aktivieren und erscheinen als eigenes Konto.
```

**Français**

```
Les calendriers partagés avec un compte, comme celui d’une équipe, s’activent avec la boîte aux lettres de l’équipe depuis la page du compte et apparaissent comme un compte à part entière.
```

**Español**

```
Los calendarios compartidos con una cuenta, como el de un equipo, se activan junto con el buzón del equipo desde la página de la cuenta y aparecen como una cuenta propia.
```

**Italiano**

```
I calendari condivisi con un account, come quello di un team, si attivano insieme alla casella di posta del team dalla pagina dell’account e compaiono come account a sé.
```

**Português**

```
Os calendários partilhados com uma conta, como o de uma equipa, ativam-se com a caixa de correio da equipa na página da conta e aparecem como uma conta própria.
```
