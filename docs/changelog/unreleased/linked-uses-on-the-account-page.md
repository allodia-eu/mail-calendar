# An account's page says where a linked calendar and address book come from

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> A use another account fills is drawn as that account, opening its page, rather than as a switch
> that could only be off; the account it comes from lists who takes it, with Stop linking; and a
> calendar that signs in separately says so on the mailbox's page once it has moved.

**English**

```
An account's settings now show which account its calendar and address book come from, and you can stop linking two accounts from the one that provides them.
```
