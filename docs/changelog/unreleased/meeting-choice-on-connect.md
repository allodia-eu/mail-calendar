# A Teams or Talk choice is offered as soon as its account connects

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> Switching an account's video meetings on, or reconnecting it, offered its Teams or Talk
> meetings in the event editor only after the next calendar sync, which takes a minute on a large
> calendar. The choice now follows the account's connection.

**English**

```
Teams and Talk meetings are now offered in the event editor as soon as their account connects.
```
