# The calendar fills in sooner at launch

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> The launch fetched the calendar only after every account's mail catch-up, body prefetch
> included; it now runs once every account has connected, beside the catch-ups.

**English**

```
After the app starts, your calendar now fills in without waiting for your mail to finish downloading.
```
