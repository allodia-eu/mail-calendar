# Say when an Allodia account sign-in has ended

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> A refresh token the account service refused reached the Allodia account screen as "could not
> check your subscription right now" under a card still reading "Signed in as". The core now answers
> `AllodiaPurchaseError::SignedOut` for it, the account card says whose sign-in ended and offers
> Sign in again and Sign out in place of the subscription section, and the connectivity snapshot
> carries it as a banner beside the mail accounts' expired sign-in prompt.

**English**

```
When your Allodia account sign-in has ended, the app now says so in a banner and offers to sign in again, instead of saying in Settings that your subscription could not be checked.
```

**Nederlands**

```
Als je aanmelding bij je Allodia-account is beëindigd, zegt de app dat nu in een melding en kun je opnieuw aanmelden, in plaats van dat Instellingen zegt dat je abonnement niet kon worden gecontroleerd.
```

