# Move mail to a folder from its menu

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> A message's row menu gains Move to folder…, which lists the folders of its account that take
> mail. On the desktops and the iPad and iPhone it moves the selection when the row is in it; on
> Android, where a long press starts a selection, it is on the row's own menu and moves that one
> message. Linux message rows now open their menu on a right click as well as from the button.

You can now move a message to any folder from its menu.
