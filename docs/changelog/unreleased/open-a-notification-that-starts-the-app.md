# A notification that starts the app opens its message

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: patch

> A click or tap that started the app reached it before the mail store had opened. With no list to look
> in, the app spent the click's one account switch on a store that could not act on it and then
> dropped it, so the app opened on the mailbox and not on the message. The click now waits for the
> store to open.

A new-mail notification now opens its message even when the app was closed.
