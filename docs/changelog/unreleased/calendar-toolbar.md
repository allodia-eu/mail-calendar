# The calendar's controls in the toolbar on Mac, iPhone and iPad

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> Laid out as Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and Apple's Calendar have it: on iPhone and iPad
> the period is the bar's title and New event and a More menu are system toolbar items; iPad and
> the Mac add the shapes as a segmented control; an iPhone turns the week and the month by a swipe.

**English**

```
The calendar's buttons now sit in the toolbar, where they are larger and easier to reach, and on iPhone the month view turns with a swipe.
```
