# A conversation's menu on macOS, iPhone and iPad

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> The row menus are drawn from the core's `row_menu`, which replaced four hand-built menus (a
> message, a conversation, a message inside one, a selection). The selection bar reads its toggles
> and what responds from the same answer. Opening a conversation goes to its newest message, and a
> conversation folds away once the selection leaves it.

**English**

```
A conversation's menu now offers what a message's does, including reply, flag and report as junk, and a conversation folds away once you open something else.
```
