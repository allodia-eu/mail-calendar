# Fetch the calendar and contacts after signing an account in again, and a new account's contacts

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> Signing an account in again caught up its mail only, so a calendar or contacts added at the
> provider's consent screen stayed empty until its tab was opened or something else fetched it. A
> new account fetched its calendar but not its contacts, so its mail showed no faces from them.
> Both now fetch the calendar and contacts beside the mail.

**English**

```
A calendar or contacts added by signing an account in again now appear straight away, as do a new account's contacts.
```
