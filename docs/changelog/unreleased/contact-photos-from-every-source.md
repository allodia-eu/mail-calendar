# Contact photos from every source of an account

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> An account connects one contacts adapter per source, and every photo was asked of the first,
> which knows only its own ids. Photos of people in a Microsoft account's organisation
> directory or a second contact folder were refused and stayed initials.

```
Photos of people in your organisation directory and in other contact folders now appear.
```
