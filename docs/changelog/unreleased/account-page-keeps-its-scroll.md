# An account's page keeps its place when a notice appears above it

Platforms: macos
Bump: patch

> Switching a use on that then failed to connect raised a notice at the top of the account's page,
> and the scroll view kept its offset, so everything below moved down by the notice's height. A
> change in that block while it is scrolled out of sight now moves the scroll view with it.

**English**

```
An account's page in Settings → Accounts no longer jumps when a notice appears at its top while you are scrolled further down.
```
