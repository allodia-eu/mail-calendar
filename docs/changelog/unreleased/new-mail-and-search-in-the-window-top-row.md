# New Mail, Sync and search where you reach for them

Platforms: macos, ios, windows, linux
Bump: minor

> Four moves, one shape, taken from Outlook because that is what people arrive from. **New Mail**
> (was "Compose") leaves the footer nobody looked at for the top of the window: on macOS and Linux a
> window-toolbar item in the leading corner, which is the only surface there that is the window's
> own top row, so the traffic lights' space is reserved by the toolbar rather than guessed at; on
> Windows the head of the actions row, because the folder pane it would otherwise sit in collapses
> to an icon strip and nothing else in the app writes a message. The search field is centred over
> the window, which is honest about its reach: the default scope is every account and every folder,
> so it was a control drawn over one column while acting on all of them. **Sync** (was "Refresh")
> joins the actions bar past a divider, never disabled, because it acts on the mailbox rather than
> on the selection; the footer it left is now a status line alone. iPhone and iPad get the gesture
> instead, and drop the menu item they had. And the unified list becomes an **All Accounts** group
> with the Inbox under it, which is the shape a unified Sent and Drafts need; its expansion is the
> core's and persisted, like every account's.
>
> The pane's rules 16 and 17 and the bar's Sync are on Apple, Windows and Linux. Android remains
> recorded under the contracts' Known gaps.

**English**

```
Search is now centred at the top of the window on Mac, Windows and Linux, and New Mail has moved up there with it. Sync sits beside Archive and Delete, and on iPhone and iPad you pull the list down to sync. Your accounts' inboxes are now one "All Accounts" group you can open and shut.
```
