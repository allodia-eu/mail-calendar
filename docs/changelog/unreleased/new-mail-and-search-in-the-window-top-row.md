# New Mail, Sync and search where you reach for them

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> Four moves, one shape, taken from Outlook because that is what people arrive from. **New Mail**
> (was "Compose") is a window-toolbar item in the leading corner: the only surface on macOS that is
> the window's own top row, so the traffic lights' space is reserved by the toolbar rather than
> guessed at. The search field is the toolbar's centred item, which is honest about its reach: the
> default scope is every account and every folder, so it was a control drawn over one column while
> acting on all of them. **Sync** (was "Refresh") joins the actions bar past a divider, never
> disabled, because it acts on the mailbox rather than on the selection; the footer it left is now a
> status line alone. iPhone and iPad get the gesture instead, and drop the menu item they had.
> And the unified list becomes an **All Accounts** group with the Inbox under it, which is the shape
> a unified Sent and Drafts need; its expansion is the core's and persisted, like every account's.
>
> The pane's rules 16 and 17 and the bar's Sync are Apple-only for now, each recorded under its
> contract's Known gaps; nothing in the core is missing for the other three clients.

**English**

```
New Mail sits in the top corner and search is centred at the top of the window, on Mac. Sync has moved up beside Archive and Delete, and on iPhone and iPad you pull the list down to sync. Your accounts' inboxes are now one "All Accounts" group you can open and shut.
```
