# Every account in Settings → Accounts on Windows, each with a page of its own

Platforms: windows
Bump: minor

> Settings → Accounts listed mail accounts only, as cards. It now lists every account from the
> core's snapshot, a calendar or contacts account included, each opening a page: what it is used
> for, the accounts it is linked to with a suggested one named, signing a Microsoft or Google
> account in again in place, a password account's servers and password tried before they are
> saved, its mail settings, and a removal that names the accounts losing their link. The main
> window's sign-in prompts now sign an account in again in place too.

**English**

```
Settings → Accounts on Windows lists every account, mail or not, each with a page to choose its uses, link it to another account, sign it in again or change its servers.
```
