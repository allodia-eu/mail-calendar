# Moving a whole repeating event keeps the occurrences you changed

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> On an account that keeps the occurrences you moved or deleted when the whole series moves
> (JMAP, CalDAV), they now move with the series. Before, a moved occurrence was drawn twice and a
> deleted one came back; and moving a second occurrence of a series on JMAP was refused by the
> server. Both were in the sync engine.

**English**

```
Moving every event of a repeating series no longer shows an event you moved separately twice, and a second event of a series can now be moved on its own.
```

