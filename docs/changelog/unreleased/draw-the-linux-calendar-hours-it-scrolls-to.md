# Keep the Linux calendar's day names on screen, and draw the hours it scrolls to

Platforms: linux
Bump: patch

> The grid paints only the events inside its viewport, and GTK moves a scrolled child without
> redrawing it, so the hours the calendar opened on stayed empty until something else caused a
> redraw. The day names and the all-day banner were painted inside the scrolled day, so opening on
> the current hour scrolled them out of sight; they are now a surface of their own above it.

```
The calendar on Linux now keeps the day names and all-day events at the top while you scroll, and shows every event in the hours you scroll to.
```
