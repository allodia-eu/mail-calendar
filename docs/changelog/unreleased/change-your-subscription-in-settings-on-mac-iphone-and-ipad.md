# Change your subscription in Settings on Mac, iPhone and iPad

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> Settings → Allodia account already drew the subscription and offered the store's own page for one
> a store bills. What it could not do was change one Allodia bills directly, which is what somebody
> who subscribed on the website or a desktop has: cancelling, moving between monthly and yearly,
> and restarting a cancellation all existed in the core and on the three other clients and were
> drawn on no Apple screen. All three are now drawn, each only where `actions` permits it, with the
> cancellation and the switch behind an inline confirmation that says what is kept and until when.
> ⚠️ **Starting a checkout is deliberately not drawn here**, unlike on Windows and Linux: it sends
> somebody to a payment page outside the app, which needs Apple's external-purchase-link
> entitlement that this build does not carry. Buying on Apple stays the App Store's.

**English**

```
On Mac, iPhone and iPad you can now cancel, restart or switch period for a subscription Allodia bills you for.
```
