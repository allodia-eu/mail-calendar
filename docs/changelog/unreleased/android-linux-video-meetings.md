# Video meetings on Android and Linux

Platforms: android, linux
Bump: minor

> The event detail draws the core's `EventDetail.conferences`: Join first, then Copy link, the
> meeting ID, the passcode and the dial-in numbers, and shows `display_notes` and
> `display_location`, without the invitation's meeting block. Blocks, all-day bars, month chips and
> agenda rows draw a camera where `has_conference` is set, and say "video meeting" to a screen
> reader.

**English**

```
An event's video meeting, from Teams, Meet, Zoom and others, can be joined from its detail, beside its meeting ID, passcode and dial-in numbers, and the calendar marks the event with a camera.
```

