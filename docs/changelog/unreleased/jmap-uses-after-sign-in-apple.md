# What a JMAP account is used for, asked after its sign-in on macOS, iPhone and iPad

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> A JMAP sign-in whose session offers more than one of mail, calendar and contacts is followed by
> the step that asks which the account is used for (`signed_in_setup_choices`), before the
> separate-server and link steps, drawn with the found card's toggles.

**English**

```
After you sign in to a JMAP account, setup now asks which of mail, calendar and contacts on its server you want to use.
```
