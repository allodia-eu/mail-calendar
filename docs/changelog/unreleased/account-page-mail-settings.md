# An account's name and mail settings follow the page's other controls

Platforms: all
Bump: patch

> The name an account sends under now sits directly under its sharing control, as the one thing on
> the page a recipient sees. Fetch depth, message size, push or poll, the interval and the watched
> folders show a change running until it is stored, and hold while another change runs.

**English**

```
In Settings, an account's name now sits at the top of its page, and its mail settings show that a change is working until it is done.
```
