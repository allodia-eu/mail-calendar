# An open message on Android names its people, keeps its actions in the top bar, and keeps the tabs

Platforms: android
Bump: minor

> The reading header gives the subject in full, then the sender's name, address and the list's date,
> and one line of recipients by name that opens every address and the full date. The actions move
> from a row under the header to the top app bar: archive and delete, the core's default answer
> (`default_reply_all`) with a chevron for the others, and the overflow, whose first item is the
> sun/moon in a dark app. The Mail, Calendar and Contacts bar stays while a message is open, and
> Mail returns to the message after a visit to another tab.

**English**

```
On Android an open message keeps the Mail, Calendar and Contacts tabs, shows its recipients by name with every address a tap away, and its actions sit in the top bar, where Reply answers everyone when the message went to several people.
```
