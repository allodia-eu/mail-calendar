# Headings, alignment and fonts in the composer, and drafts from Thunderbird and Outlook

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> The composer gains headings, alignment, paragraph indentation, horizontal lines and a font picker,
> and keeps a size at the exact size it was set. A draft started in Thunderbird or Outlook reopens
> with the formatting, quote and signature they show, and one saved here opens there the same way.
