# The invitation card's answer buttons take a click across their whole face

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: patch

> The meeting-day preview slides a whole day up behind a clip, and a SwiftUI clip crops only the
> drawing: a block earlier in the day lay unseen over Accept, Maybe and Decline and took their
> clicks. The preview no longer takes clicks at all.

**English**

```
Accept, Maybe and Decline on a meeting invitation now respond wherever you click them, not only on part of the button.
```

**Nederlands**

```
Accepteren, Misschien en Afwijzen in een uitnodiging reageren nu waar je ook op de knop klikt, niet alleen op een deel ervan.
```
