# Switch how an account signs in

Platforms: all
Bump: minor

> A mail account can move between a password and signing in with its provider from its page in
> Settings → Accounts, keeping its mail, settings and links; an account that signs in with its
> provider can sign in again there; and adding an account that is already set up offers to update
> it instead.

**English**

```
A mail account can now switch between a password and signing in with your provider in Settings, keeping its mail and settings.
```
