# Cancel, restart or change your subscription from Settings

Platforms: android
Bump: minor

> The three writes [`allodia_license/purchasing.md`](../../../allodia_license/purchasing.md) has
> carried in the core since it was written, now reaching a screen for the first time. They act on
> the subscription Allodia bills directly and never on a store's, which is changed at that store and
> nowhere else, and each appears only where `actions` says it may: the service computed that with
> every biller in view, which no client is in a position to do. Both of the two that change what
> somebody is charged ask first, because neither says anything anywhere else until a date that may
> be a month away. The cancellation names the day access runs to, or it reads as "it stops now",
> which is the one thing it does not do; its dismiss button says "Keep subscription" rather than
> "Cancel", because on that dialog the word is the question. The switch quotes **no** price, because
> what a subscriber is charged is not today's list price and a price change never reaches somebody
> who already subscribed; the amount comes back from the switch itself and is said afterwards.
> ⚠️ Drawn but unexercised: none of the three has yet been run against a real subscription.
