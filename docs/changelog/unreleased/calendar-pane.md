# The calendar has a pane of its own

Platforms: macos, ios
Bump: minor

> On a desktop, and on an iPad at full width, the pane beside the calendar shows a mini month and
> every calendar under its account in place of the folder tree, with Mail, Calendar, Contacts and
> Settings pinned beneath. The calendar manager dialog is gone there; a phone keeps it.

**English**

```
Beside the calendar, on a Mac and on an iPad, a small month moves the calendar to any day, and every calendar can be shown, hidden or recoloured where it is listed. Mail now has its own button under the pane.
```

