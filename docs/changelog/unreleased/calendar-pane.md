# The calendar has a pane of its own

Platforms: macos, ios, linux
Bump: minor

> On a desktop, and on a tablet at full width, the pane beside the calendar shows a mini month and
> every calendar under its account in place of the folder tree, with Mail, Calendar, Contacts and
> Settings pinned beneath. The calendar manager dialog is gone there; a phone keeps it.

**English**

```
On a wide screen, a small month beside the calendar moves it to any day, and every calendar can be shown, hidden or recoloured where it is listed, with Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Settings pinned underneath.
```

