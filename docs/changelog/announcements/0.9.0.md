# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.9.0

Released 13 September 2026.

This release is mostly about reading a message. A newsletter written for a wider screen now reflows to fit the reading pane instead of running off the right edge, and you can pinch to zoom any message. New Mail and the search field have moved to the top row of the window on Mac, Windows and Linux, which is where most people reach for them. Windows gains new-mail notifications, and on iPhone, Android and Linux a notification now shows how the message begins. The rest is listed below.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows and Android

### ✨ New

- **Newsletters reflow to the reading pane.** A newsletter written for a wider screen now reflows to fit the reading pane instead of running off the right edge, at whatever width you read it.
- **Save a message as an .eml file.** An open message can now be saved as an .eml file, from the new menu at the end of the reading toolbar.
- **Search follows conversation grouping.** Search results are now grouped into conversations when conversation grouping is on.
- **You can zoom a message.** You can now pinch to zoom a message, and every message opens at its own scale again.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A French folder badge of one reads as one.** A folder holding one unread message now reads as a singular in French.
- **Counts of one read as one.** A list or a selection holding one thing now reads "1 conversation" rather than "1 conversations".
- **Forwarding keeps the attachments.** Forwarding a message now brings its attachments into the composer, where you can remove any you would rather not pass on.
- **JMAP setup that moves to another server.** Mail accounts on JMAP providers that hand setup to a separate server, such as Thundermail, can now be added.
- **Refreshing a folder you opened yourself.** Mail arriving in a folder you opened yourself now shows up on a refresh, and message previews appear as soon as the bodies are downloaded.
- **Responsive newsletters on a phone.** Newsletters that adapt to a small screen now do so in the reading view, instead of being cut off at the right edge on a phone.
- **Search answers the query you finished typing.** Search results no longer flicker between the right answer and an unrelated list while you are still typing.
- **Subjects in other alphabets read correctly.** Subjects and sender names in alphabets such as Japanese now read correctly; mail synced earlier corrects itself on the next full sync.

## 🖥️ macOS

- **Clear a search from the field on macOS.** Leave a search on macOS with the clear button in the search field.
- **New Mail, Sync and search where you reach for them.** Search is now centred at the top of the window on Mac, Windows and Linux, and New Mail has moved up there with it. Sync sits beside Archive and Delete, and on iPhone and iPad you pull the list down to sync. Your accounts' inboxes are now one "All Accounts" group you can open and shut.

---

## 📱 iPhone & iPad

### ✨ New

- **New Mail, Sync and search where you reach for them.** Search is now centred at the top of the window on Mac, Windows and Linux, and New Mail has moved up there with it. Sync sits beside Archive and Delete, and on iPhone and iPad you pull the list down to sync. Your accounts' inboxes are now one "All Accounts" group you can open and shut.
- **Notifications show how the message begins.** New-mail notifications now show how the message begins, after the sender and the subject.
- **The message header scrolls with the message.** On iPhone and iPad the sender, recipients and attachments now scroll away with the message, leaving reply, archive and delete in place at the top.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A steadier mailbox screen on the phone.** Pulling down to sync on the mailbox list no longer hides the search field or jolts the messages, and the list's menu is gone: both of its options are in Settings.
- **Pinch the calendar on iPhone and iPad.** Pinching the calendar on iPhone and iPad zooms it again, both the hours and the days.
- **The open message fits the screen.** An open message now fits the screen on iPhone: the date, the actions and the message itself no longer run off the edges.

## 🪟 Windows

### ✨ New

- **New Mail, Sync and search where you reach for them.** Search is now centred at the top of the window on Mac, Windows and Linux, and New Mail has moved up there with it. Sync sits beside Archive and Delete, and on iPhone and iPad you pull the list down to sync. Your accounts' inboxes are now one "All Accounts" group you can open and shut.
- **New-mail notifications on Windows.** New mail now raises a desktop notification, showing who it is from, what it is about and how the message begins. Turn it off in Settings → Notifications.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **An untitled message is headed in your own language on Windows.** A message with no subject is now headed in your own language, not in English.
- **Collapsing the sidebar keeps your folder trees.** Collapsing the folder pane no longer shuts every account's folders for good: the trees you left open are open again when you bring the pane back.
- **The default-mail-app offer no longer crashes the Windows app at startup.** Fixed a crash at startup, on the launch where Allodia Mail & Calendar offers to become your default mail app.

## 🤖 Android

- **Notifications show how the message begins.** New-mail notifications now show how the message begins, after the sender and the subject.

## 🐧 Linux (not yet in a store)

### ✨ New

- **New Mail, Sync and search where you reach for them.** Search is now centred at the top of the window on Mac, Windows and Linux, and New Mail has moved up there with it. Sync sits beside Archive and Delete, and on iPhone and iPad you pull the list down to sync. Your accounts' inboxes are now one "All Accounts" group you can open and shut.
- **Notifications show how the message begins.** New-mail notifications now show how the message begins, after the sender and the subject.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **Notifications skip the catch-up a launch begins with.** Starting the app no longer announces the mail that arrived while it was closed: notifications are for mail that arrives while you have it open.
- **What changed, under every version in your software centre.** Your software centre now shows what changed in each release, and lists the Linux app as one that works with a touchscreen.
- **Window buttons in the corner.** The buttons that minimise, maximise and close the window sit in the top corner again, and the application's name no longer stands above the message you are reading.

The Linux app is not in a store yet, so there is nowhere to update from. The above is what landed this cycle.
