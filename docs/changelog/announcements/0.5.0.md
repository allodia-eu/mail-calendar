# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.5.0

Released 20 August 2026.

This is a big release: 63 changes. The headline additions are a face beside every person (their photo where your account has one, coloured initials where it does not), contacts carried across from the accounts you sign in to, drag-to-edit on the calendar grid, and a folder pane that stays exactly as you left it. Under that sits a long list of sync, sending and start-up fixes.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows and Android

### ✨ New

- **A quiet note when mail is catching up.** When mail is arriving in the background, a quiet note now says which account is catching up and how far along it is, instead of a progress bar appearing over your list.
- **Accounts you sign in to can read contacts.** Contacts now come across from accounts you sign in to.
- **An About page, on every client.** Settings now has an About page: the version you're running, a link to our support forum, and the open-source work the app is built on.
- **An answer that stops claiming to have been delivered when it was not.** When your calendar server can't pass your invitation answer to the organiser, you're now told, and offered to email it yourself.
- **Answering an invitation works on an account whose calendar server will not answer for you.** Meeting invitations can now be answered on accounts whose calendar server doesn't send replies for you: the meeting is added to your calendar and your answer is emailed to the organiser.
- **Choose light or dark yourself.** Settings now let you keep the app light or dark whatever your device is set to. It still follows your system unless you pick one.
- **Keeping less mail frees the space right away.** Choosing to keep less mail on this device now removes the older mail and the space it used straight away, even with no connection.
- **Known folders get their proper names.** Inbox, Drafts, Sent, Archive, Junk and Trash now show those names instead of whatever your server calls them (so no more shouting INBOX), and they follow the app's language.
- **Nested bullets, table rows and columns, and text colour in the composer.** The composer can now nest bullet points (Tab and Shift+Tab change the level), add and remove table rows and columns, and set text and highlight colour.
- **Search says how far back it looked.** Search results now say how far back they looked, and link straight to the setting that changes it.
- **Senders and contacts get a face.** Senders and contacts now show their photo where your account has one, and coloured initials where it does not, in place of the envelope icon.
- **The folder pane stays as you left it, and shows what is waiting.** Your folder pane now stays open the way you left it, including after a restart, and shows unread counts per folder, with icons for Inbox, Sent, Archive, Junk and Trash.
- **The older copy of a re-sent meeting stops pretending it can still be answered.** When an organiser re-sends a meeting, the older invitation now says it is out of date and offers no answer, so you can't agree to times that have changed.
- **Typing "- " at the start of a line starts a bullet list.** Start a line with "- " and it turns into a bullet list, the way it does in Word and Outlook.
- **Your calendar is there when the app opens.** Your calendar is on screen the moment the app opens, and it keeps itself up to date whether or not you visit it, so your diary is there when you have no connection.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A flag change no longer refetches the message.** Reading, flagging or filing mail on another device now syncs as just that change, so it arrives faster and uses far less data.
- **A forward stays in the conversation it came from.** A message you forward now stays in the conversation it came from, instead of starting a new one.
- **A message with many files stays readable.** A message with a lot of attachments no longer hides its own text: the file list keeps to a few rows and scrolls, so the message and every file stay reachable.
- **A message you open stays in its conversation.** Opening a message no longer bumps it out of its conversation for a moment, and conversations open faster in large mailboxes.
- **A network failure while renewing a sign-in no longer costs you the sign-in.** A brief network problem while an account renewed its sign-in could revoke that sign-in and make you sign in again. Fixed.
- **A refused password now says so, instead of blaming the server.** An account whose password or token the server has stopped accepting now tells you to sign in again, instead of claiming its server can't be reached.
- **A sent message that left no copy in Sent.** Messages you send from an IMAP account now reliably reach your Sent folder, and if a copy still can't be saved, the app tells you and offers to save it again instead of losing it quietly.
- **A swipe no longer wakes every account.** Archiving, deleting or marking a message read now refreshes only the account it belongs to, instead of contacting every account's server.
- **An invitation now shows on its own day preview.** The day preview under an invitation now shows the meeting itself, dotted, even when your server has not put it in your calendar yet.
- **Conversations keep up without rescanning the mailbox.** Conversations now group as mail arrives, so syncing after a change is much faster on large mailboxes.
- **Every recipient of a reply-all, drawn as a recipient.** Reply all now shows every recipient as its own pill, instead of leaving the last one as loose text.
- **Folder names with an ampersand or non-Latin characters.** Folder names containing an ampersand or non-Latin characters now display correctly.
- **IMAP accounts re-index their folders once.** IMAP accounts re-index their folders once after this update, and the app now uses the newer IMAP protocol with servers that support it.
- **Mail arriving in the background no longer loses an account's renewed sign-in.** An account could quietly lose its renewed sign-in while mail arrived in the background, then ask you to sign in again a day later. Fixed, and signing back in now sticks.
- **New mail arrives with less waiting.** New mail pushed by your server now appears faster, and a full sync does less redundant work.
- **One refused folder no longer asks you to sign in again.** The app no longer asks you to sign in again when a server refuses one folder while the rest of the account keeps working. It asks only when nothing on the account is getting through.
- **Removing an account now really removes it, and one that can't be saved says so.** Removing an account now erases its saved sign-in as part of the removal, so it can’t reappear the next time you open the app, and an account whose sign-in can’t be saved says so instead of quietly disappearing later.
- **The account list stays put while the app starts.** Your accounts no longer reshuffle themselves in the sidebar while the app starts up. They stay in the order you added them.
- **The app stays responsive during a first sync.** A first sync no longer makes the app stutter: opening a message stays instant while mail downloads, and the status line now says when message bodies are still coming in.
- **The composer spell-checked every message against English.** The message editor no longer spell-checks everything as English. It follows the language you are actually writing in.
- **The list no longer jumps when a background sync starts.** The message list no longer jumps when a background sync starts or finishes.
- **The mail list opens at once, however much mail you have.** The message list now opens instantly and stays fast as your mailbox grows, on every account.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **Drag on the calendar grid to create, move and resize.** Drag on the calendar to draw out a new event, or to move and resize one you own. A repeating event asks whether you meant this one or all of them.
- **The composer opens on the fields a message usually needs.** Writing a message now starts with just From, To and Subject. Cc and Bcc are one tap away, and open by themselves when there is already someone in them.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A long address no longer pushes the composer off the screen.** A recipient longer than the field now shortens to fit, instead of pushing the whole composer sideways off the screen.
- **The Mac app goes by its own name.** The Mac app now calls itself Allodia Mail & Calendar in the menu bar and in Finder, and the window no longer repeats its own name above the account list.
- **The calendar drag on Mac and iPhone works the way it does on Android.** Drawing an event on the Mac and iPhone calendar now behaves the way it does on Android: it fills the hour you pressed in, follows your finger smoothly, and shows the time in a floating label.
- **The editor's own buttons now speak the app's language on macOS, iOS and Windows.** The formatting toolbar in the message editor now follows the app's language on macOS, iPhone, iPad and Windows.

### ✨ New

- **Drag on the calendar grid to create, move and resize.** Drag on the calendar to draw out a new event, or to move and resize one you own. A repeating event asks whether you meant this one or all of them.
- **The composer opens on the fields a message usually needs.** Writing a message now starts with just From, To and Subject. Cc and Bcc are one tap away, and open by themselves when there is already someone in them.

- **No shadow down the edge of a closed folder pane.** The folder pane no longer leaves a faint shadow down the side of your mail when it is closed.

- **The editor's own buttons now speak the app's language on macOS, iOS and Windows.** The formatting toolbar in the message editor now follows the app's language on macOS, iPhone, iPad and Windows.

## 🪟 Windows

### ✨ New

- **Mail links open in the app.** Mail links now open in Allodia Mail & Calendar. Choose it as your email app, and clicking an email address on a web page, or choosing to send something by email from another app, opens a new message with the address and subject already filled in. Nothing is sent until you write your message and press Send, and everything the link filled in stays editable.
- **The calendar scrolls like one long strip of days.** Scrolling the calendar sideways with a mouse or trackpad is now smooth and continuous, and the grid comes to rest on a day instead of jumping to a whole week.
- **The composer opens on the fields a message usually needs.** Writing a message now starts with just From, To and Subject. Cc and Bcc are one tap away, and open by themselves when there is already someone in them.
- **The folder pane can be widened.** You can now drag the folder pane wider, so long account addresses fit, and it stays where you put it.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **Opening another message no longer flickers.** Opening another message no longer makes the reading pane flicker. The message you picked appears in one step.
- **The editor's own buttons now speak the app's language on macOS, iOS and Windows.** The formatting toolbar in the message editor now follows the app's language on macOS, iPhone, iPad and Windows.
- **The empty strip across the top is gone.** The empty band above the folder name, the search box and the reading pane is gone, so more mail fits on screen.

## 🤖 Android

- **Drag on the calendar grid to create, move and resize.** Drag on the calendar to draw out a new event, or to move and resize one you own. A repeating event asks whether you meant this one or all of them.

## 🐧 Linux (in development, not yet released)

### ✨ New

- **Contacts on Linux, under a switcher that stays put.** Linux gains a Contacts screen with everyone in your accounts' address books, A–Z with search. Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Settings now sit pinned under the folder list, where a long list of folders can no longer push them off screen.
- **Conversations open in place on Linux, and unread mail is bold.** Conversations now open where they sit in the list, showing every message on the thread, your own replies included, and unread mail stands out in bold.
- **Linux account setup matches the other apps.** Adding an account on Linux now works the way it does on the other apps: sign in with Microsoft or Google in your browser, confirm the servers we found instead of retyping them, and pick your account type when you set one up by hand.
- **Mail actions on Linux.** Linux now lets you mark messages read or unread, flag them, archive them, move them to Trash, mark them as spam, or delete them permanently.
- **Meeting invitations on Linux.** Linux now shows meeting invitations as a card above the message: who is asking, when, where, how everyone answered, and your calendar at that time. You can Accept, Maybe or Decline without leaving the email.
- **Search your mail on Linux.** Linux gains mail search: one field over the list finds messages across every account and folder, newest first, with a filter to narrow to what you were looking at and a line saying how far back the results reached.
- **Signatures on Linux.** Linux gains signatures: write one in Settings, with formatting and a logo, choose one per account for new messages and for replies, and swap or drop it for a single message in the composer.
- **The Linux composer completes recipients, and draws them as pills.** The composer now completes recipients as you type, from your contacts and from people you have written to, and shows each finished address as a chip you can remove.
- **The Linux folder pane catches up with the other apps.** The folder pane now shows every account's folders at once, opens and shuts each account independently and remembers that after a restart, badges unread counts, and calls Inbox, Sent, Archive, Junk and Trash by those names, with their own icons. Drag its edge to widen it.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **Settings lists every language the app ships in.** Settings now offers every language the app ships in: German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese as well as English and Dutch.

The Linux app cannot be installed yet: it is not on Flathub and much of it is still unfinished. The above is what landed this cycle, listed for completeness.
