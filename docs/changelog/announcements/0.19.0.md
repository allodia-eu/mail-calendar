# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.19.0

Released 21 November 2026.

This release brings message importance, a calendar that keeps your place, and a long list of fixes! 🚀

- **Message importance**: messages sent with high or low importance are marked in the list and say so when opened, and you can set the importance of a message you write from the composer's action bar. Available on every app.
- **A calendar that keeps your place**: switching between week and month keeps the day you were looking at, the grid stays in view while a period loads, paging is instant again, and weeks far back or a year ahead now load. Available on every app.
- **A pane beside the calendar**: a small month takes you to any day, and each calendar can be shown, hidden or recoloured where it is listed. On macOS, iPad, Windows and Linux.
- **Writing and answering mail**: an invitation addressed to several people is answered from your own address, a message you open and leave untouched is no longer saved to Drafts, and a signature's pictures reach every recipient. Available on every app.
- **New-mail notifications on Windows**: the app from the Microsoft Store or our website now notifies you of new mail.

**The latest version is available through our website: [https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar](https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar)**

**Store availability**: ⏳ this version is in review in the stores; this line changes to ✅ once it is live on all supported platforms.

The changes below are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **Message importance: read it, and send it.** Messages sent with high or low importance are now marked in the list and say so when opened, and you can set a message’s importance while writing it.
- **The calendar keeps one day in focus across its views.** The calendar remembers the day you are looking at: switching between week and month keeps your place, and the selected day is marked.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A Thunderbird quote wraps in the reading pane.** Quoted text in messages from Thunderbird now wraps to the width of the reading pane instead of running off its edge.
- **A calendar page more than a few months back or a year ahead loads.** Calendar weeks and months more than four months back or a year ahead now load, instead of showing "Loading this period…".
- **A composer nobody wrote in is no longer saved to Drafts.** A message you open and leave untouched, including a reply or a draft from another app, is no longer saved to Drafts on its own.
- **A new message comes from an account used for mail.** A new message is never written from an account used only for its calendar or contacts, and Settings offers a default send account only when there is more than one to choose from.
- **An IPv6 address works as a mail server.** A mail server given as an IPv6 address, such as [::1]:993, is now accepted in setup and on an account's page.
- **Answering an invitation answers as you, not as another attendee.** Answering a meeting invitation addressed to several people now replies from your own address, so the answer no longer stays in the Outbox.
- **Attach shows that chosen files are still on their way.** The attach button now shows that it is working while files you chose, such as a video from your photo library, are still being prepared.
- **Calendar and video meeting settings keep up with the calendars.** The default calendar and video meeting settings now keep up as calendars arrive or change, and a calendar list with one account no longer repeats its address.
- **Paging the calendar no longer pauses.** Moving to the next or previous week or month in the calendar is instant again.
- **Settings are saved without holding up the window, and a failed save is said.** Changing a setting no longer waits on saving it, and if a setting can’t be saved you are told and can try again.
- **Settings → Advanced says what a reset and assistant access came to.** Resetting the local database now keeps Settings open and says whether it worked, and AI assistant access says when another copy of the app is already serving assistants.
- **Signatures are saved by the core, once, and take the pictures every mail app shows.** Saving a signature now shows that it is working and cannot add it twice, and its pictures are PNG, JPEG or GIF up to 512 KB on every device, so every recipient sees them.
- **Signing in from an account's settings at the server setup found.** For an email address on a domain of your own, signing in through your provider from an account’s settings now uses the same sign-in server as adding the account.
- **The calendar keeps its grid while a period loads.** While the calendar loads a period, its grid stays in view with a spinner over it.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **The calendar has a pane of its own.** On a wide screen, a small month beside the calendar moves it to any day, and every calendar can be shown, hidden or recoloured where it is listed, with Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Settings pinned underneath.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A long sender no longer pushes the Mac composer off its pane.** In a narrow window, a long sender name no longer pushes the message you are writing off the edges of its pane.
- **An event's invitee suggestions stay inside the editor on a Mac.** When you invite people to an event, the address suggestions no longer run under Cancel and Save.
- **Printing works in the Mac App Store build.** Printing a message works again in the version from the Mac App Store, which answered that the application does not support printing.

## 🪟 Windows

### ✨ New

- **The calendar has a pane of its own.** On a wide screen, a small month beside the calendar moves it to any day, and every calendar can be shown, hidden or recoloured where it is listed, with Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Settings pinned underneath.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **New-mail notifications on the installed Windows app.** New mail now raises a notification on Windows when the app is installed from the Microsoft Store or our website.
- **The Windows calendar's header names the week after the month.** After paging the month view, switching to a week or day view shows that period's name in the header again.
- **The Windows calendar's week opens on its first day after the agenda or the month.** Opening the week view after the agenda or month view starts the week on its first day again, not on today.
- **The calendar's arrows count every press on Windows.** Pressing the calendar’s previous or next arrow several times quickly now moves one period per press and lines the days up.

## 🤖 Android

- **The Android calendar's week opens on its first day after the month.** Switching from the month view to the week view starts the week on its first day again, and the month view opens on the current month.

## 🐧 Linux

### ✨ New

- **The calendar has a pane of its own.** On a wide screen, a small month beside the calendar moves it to any day, and every calendar can be shown, hidden or recoloured where it is listed, with Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Settings pinned underneath.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **Linux frees what a closed composer, window or signature editor held, and a new composer always draws.** Closing a message you were writing, a message window or the signature editor, or printing a message, now frees its memory, and a new message no longer opens without its writing area.
- **Linux's manual setup no longer says it is still checking.** Setting an account up by hand no longer keeps saying it is checking how you can sign in after the server has answered.
- **The reading spinner keeps its size on Linux.** The indicator shown while a message loads is now a small spinner in the middle of the reading pane, rather than one that filled it.
