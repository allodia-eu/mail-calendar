# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.10.0

Released 22 September 2026.

This release carries 42 changes, and three of them are what it is about. Folders inside other folders are now drawn inside them, in every app and on Microsoft accounts too. A message that cannot be sent waits in the Outbox and goes out by itself when the connection returns, instead of being lost. And contacts from a Google account now appear in the app. Beside those: on the desktop a message opens in a window of its own, and mail software running on your own machine, such as Proton Mail Bridge, can be connected.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows and Android

### ✨ New

- **A folder inside a folder is drawn inside it.** Folders inside other folders now show inside them, with their own arrow to open and shut each one, remembered the next time you open the app.
- **A message that cannot be sent waits in the Outbox.** A message that cannot be sent right away now waits in the Outbox and goes out by itself when the connection returns, instead of being lost; you can send it now, edit it, or cancel it.
- **An empty folder says why it is empty.** A folder that looks empty now says whether it really is empty or whether older mail is still on the server, with a link to change how far back this account syncs.
- **Contacts from a Google account.** Contacts from a Google account now appear in the app, including the addresses Google saved for you and, on a work account, your colleagues.
- **Mail software on your own machine can be connected.** Mail software running on your own machine, such as Proton Mail Bridge, can now be connected: the certificate it identifies itself with is shown to you, and the account connects once you accept it.
- **Resize a picture in the message by dragging a corner.** You can now resize a picture in a message by dragging one of its corners, with a mouse, a trackpad or your finger, and the picture keeps its proportions.
- **Set a server up by hand, with its port and its security.** Setting a server up by hand now offers its port and its connection security beside the server name, so a server that uses STARTTLS, or any port other than the usual one, can be set up.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A folder that failed to download tries again when you open it.** A folder whose mail could not be downloaded when you opened it now tries again the next time you open it, instead of staying empty until you restart the app.
- **A quoted plain-text message keeps its line breaks.** Replying to a message written in plain text no longer squashes the quoted conversation into one paragraph; its line breaks and quote levels are kept.
- **A resized picture keeps its size in the recipient's client.** A picture you resized now arrives at that size in the recipient's mail app, and one you did not resize no longer runs off the side of a narrow window.
- **A sender name set on a Google account reaches Gmail.** The sender name you set for a Google account you connect from now on is also saved to Gmail, so your other apps send under the same name.
- **A server asking us to slow down is no longer reported as an outage.** When a mail provider asks the app to slow down, the app now waits as long as it was asked and stops showing "can't reach the server" for an account whose server answered perfectly well.
- **Answering an invitation on a Microsoft account.** Accepting, declining or answering "maybe" to an invitation now works on a Microsoft account, instead of reporting that the answer was not sent.
- **Archiving applies instead of returning the message to the list.** Archiving, deleting or flagging a message now applies reliably, instead of the row leaving the list and returning a moment later.
- **Folders inside folders on a Microsoft account.** A Microsoft account now shows the folders filed inside other folders, and the mail in them, instead of only the folders at the top of the mailbox.
- **No "your name" step where the provider already knows the name.** Connecting an account no longer asks for your name when your provider already knows it; the app takes the name you have there.
- **Signing in no longer briefly reports that you are signed out.** Signing in to your Allodia account no longer briefly reports that you're signed out.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **Change your subscription in Settings on Mac, iPhone and iPad.** On Mac, iPhone and iPad you can now cancel, restart or switch period for a subscription Allodia bills you for.
- **New-mail notifications on macOS.** Mail arriving while Allodia Mail & Calendar is open now raises a notification on macOS, saying who it is from, what it is about and how it begins. Clicking one opens that message. Turn it off in Settings → Notifications.
- **Open a message in its own window on macOS.** Double-click a message on macOS to open it in a window of its own, and reply or forward from there in a window of its own too.
- **See your subscription in Settings on macOS, iOS and Android.** Settings now shows your subscription: what you're being charged, by whom, when it renews, and where to change or cancel it.
- **Share a file into a message on Mac, iPhone and iPad.** Send a file by email straight from the app you are in: share a photo, a document or a link, and a message opens with it already attached.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **Assistant access on macOS.** Assistant access now starts on macOS instead of quietly staying off after it was switched on.
- **Calendar, Contacts and Settings stay in reach on Apple.** Calendar, Contacts and Settings now stay at the bottom of the folder pane instead of below your last folder, and your accounts and folders stay on screen while the calendar or contacts is open, so the unified Inbox is always one click away.
- **Select more than one line of the diagnostic log.** You can now select and copy more than one line at a time in Settings → Diagnostics.
- **Signing in with Google on macOS.** Signing in with Google now works on macOS; it used to stop with "Operation not permitted" before the browser opened.
- **The folder you are reading is highlighted again on Apple.** The folder pane now shows which folder you are reading: the row is highlighted, where before nothing in the pane said where you were.

## 🪟 Windows

### ✨ New

- **Open a message in its own window on Windows.** Double-click a message on Windows to open it in a window of its own, and reply or forward from there in a window of its own too.
- **See and change your subscription in Settings on Windows.** Settings now shows your subscription on Windows: what you pay, when it renews, and where Allodia bills you directly, the buttons to cancel it, start it again or move between monthly and yearly.
- **Settings says what keeps this copy up to date, and checks when asked.** Settings → About now says what keeps your copy up to date, and checks for a new version when you ask it to.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A subscription change that was refused says why.** When a change to your subscription can’t be made, Settings now says why instead of showing a code.
- **An invitation card follows the appearance setting.** A meeting invitation is now readable when the app's appearance differs from the desktop's, instead of drawing its labels in near-white on a light card.
- **The "couldn't open the app" message is in your language.** The message shown when the app can’t finish starting is now translated.
- **The Windows message list says nothing under the pointer.** Moving the mouse down the message list no longer trails a "Delete" label across the messages being scanned.
- **Windows message windows carry the app's own caption.** A message or draft opened in its own window now carries the app's own title bar, and the window buttons line up with it instead of sitting above it.

## 🐧 Linux (not yet in a store)

### ✨ New

- **Open a message in its own window on Linux.** Double-click a message to open it in a window of its own, and reply or forward from there in a window of its own too.
- **See and change your subscription in Settings on Linux.** Settings now shows your subscription: what you’re being charged and until when, and lets you subscribe, cancel, start again or move between monthly and yearly.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A subscription change that was refused says why.** When a change to your subscription can’t be made, Settings now says why instead of showing a code.
- **Calendar opens on the current hour, scrolls, and survives a resize.** The calendar now opens at the current hour and scrolls from the moment it appears, instead of standing at midnight and ignoring the wheel until you moved to another week, and resizing the window keeps the hours you were reading rather than dropping you back at the start of the day.
- **Message windows on Linux are peers of the mailbox.** A message opened in its own window can now be moved behind the mailbox, and the desktop lists it under the app, so the window switcher moves between them.
- **Paste a picture into a message, and into a signature.** Pasting a picture into a message or a signature now puts it in place, instead of doing nothing, whether you copied the picture itself or the file it is in.

The Linux app is not in a store yet, so there is nowhere to update from. The above is what landed this cycle.
