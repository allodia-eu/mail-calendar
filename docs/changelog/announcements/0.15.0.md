# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.15.0

Released 2 November 2026.

This release carries 27 changes, and three of them are what it is about. Mailboxes shared with your account, such as a team's, can now be added in Settings → Accounts on every app, including on Microsoft 365; each appears as a mailbox of its own and sends from its own address. A conversation in the message list now has a menu offering what a message's does, and an action on a whole conversation reaches only the messages it should, leaving your drafts where they are. And Gmail accounts sync over IMAP, so a mailbox of a thousand messages arrives in seconds rather than minutes. Beside those: an event's video meeting can be joined with one tap on macOS, iPhone and iPad, and a phone number can be linked in a message or signature.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **Gmail mail synced over IMAP.** Gmail accounts sync faster: a mailbox of a thousand messages now arrives in seconds rather than minutes.
- **Sync technology on a Gmail account's page.** A Gmail account’s page in Settings now shows its sync technology, Optimised or Fallback, and says when a network keeps its mail from arriving.
- **Telephone links in the composer.** Link a phone number so that tapping it starts a call, in a message or in your signature.
- **What an action on a whole conversation reaches.** Marking a whole conversation read now touches only the messages you received, flagging it flags the newest of those, and archiving or deleting it leaves your drafts in place.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A calendar or address book the server does not have is said once.** When a server has no calendar or address book for your account, its page now says so instead of reporting the server as unreachable.
- **Calendar and contacts servers behind a TLS-terminating proxy.** Calendar and contacts servers such as Nextcloud now connect, still encrypted, when their address redirects to an unencrypted one, and a server address with a path is used as you entered it.
- **Change how much mail to keep while an account is still downloading.** Choosing to keep more mail while an account is still downloading now fetches the older mail too.
- **Contact photos from every source of an account.** Photos of people in your organisation directory and in other contact folders now appear.
- **Fewer sign-ins when the network status flickers.** Mail accounts sign in to their servers far less often on a device whose network status keeps changing, so servers no longer limit them for it.
- **Gmail's inbox tabs, chat and extra stars are not folders.** A Gmail account’s folder list no longer shows its inbox tabs, chat and extra stars as folders with names like CATEGORY_SOCIAL; that mail is still in the Inbox or All Mail.
- **One All Mail per account, and Important named in your language.** A Gmail account’s folder list shows All Mail once, and Important in your language rather than as IMPORTANT.
- **Remove an account without waiting for the store to be compacted.** Removing an account now takes it off screen straight away; the space it used is freed afterwards, in the background.
- **Save drafts with attachments without writing them again at every step.** Saving a draft with attachments writes much less to disk, and a draft or message is no longer kept on the device a second time once the server has it.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **A conversation's menu on macOS, iPhone and iPad.** A conversation's menu now offers what a message's does, including reply, flag and report as junk; a flagged conversation shows its flag, and a conversation folds away once you open something else.
- **Shared mailboxes on macOS and iOS.** Mailboxes shared with your account, such as a team's, including on Microsoft 365, can be added in Settings → Accounts. Each appears as a mailbox of its own and sends from its own address.
- **Video meetings on macOS, iPhone and iPad.** An event's video meeting, from Teams, Meet, Zoom and others, can be joined from its detail with one tap, beside its meeting ID, passcode and dial-in numbers, and the calendar marks the event with a camera.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A draft opens in its composer wherever it is listed, on macOS, iPhone and iPad.** A draft opens in its composer wherever you find it, such as inside a conversation in your inbox, not only in Drafts.
- **Google sign-in on a desktop no longer fails when the browser asks for more than the redirect.** Signing in with Google on a computer no longer fails after you have approved it in the browser.
- **The reading pane moves on to a draft as a click would.** After you archive or delete a message, the next one opens as it would from the list, so a draft opens for editing.
