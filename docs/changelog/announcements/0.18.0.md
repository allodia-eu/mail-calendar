# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.18.0

Released 16 November 2026.

This release brings a tidier composer, calendars shared with you and video meetings by default! 🚀

- **Files above the message**: the files attached to a message are listed above it, with a small preview of each picture and their total size, and the whole composer scrolls as one page. On macOS, Windows and Linux the formatting toolbar keeps to one row and stays in view; on iPhone and iPad it sits on the keyboard. Available on every app.
- **Calendars shared with you**: a calendar shared with an account, such as a team's, can be switched on from the account's page together with the team's mailbox, and appears as an account of its own. Available on every app.
- **Video meetings by default**: choose a default video meeting for each account, and add Google Meet to an event on any calendar through your Google account. On macOS, iPhone, iPad and Windows you can also choose which calendar a new event goes in. Available on every app.
- **Photos on iPhone and iPad**: attach photos and videos from your photo library, and share a photo or file to the app to start a new message.

**The latest version is available through our website: [https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar](https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar)**

**Store availability**: ⏳ this version is in review in the stores; this line changes to ✅ once it is live on all supported platforms.

The changes below are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **A composer's files show a picture of themselves and their total size.** Attached pictures show a small preview, long file names keep their ending, and the number of files and their total size are shown above them.
- **A default video meeting per account.** Choose a default video meeting for each account under Settings → Videoconferencing: a new event gets it as soon as you invite someone.
- **An account's page says where a linked calendar and address book come from.** An account's settings now show which account its calendar and address book come from, and you can stop linking two accounts from the one that provides them.
- **Google Meet through a Google account.** Add a Google Meet meeting to an event on any calendar through your Google account, once Video meetings is on for it in Settings.
- **Open a calendar shared with you.** Calendars shared with an account, such as a team's, can be switched on from the account's page together with the team's mailbox, and appear as an account of their own.
- **The composer lists its files above the message and scrolls as one page.** The files attached to a message are listed above it with their size, and the whole composer scrolls as one page, so every file stays in reach however many you add.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A blocked image leaves an empty space.** While remote images are blocked, a message now leaves an empty space where each one goes, rather than a broken-image icon.
- **A calendar on a server of its own signs in through the browser the same way everywhere.** Signing a separate calendar server in through your browser now shows that it is working, can be cancelled, and can be started again if you left the browser.
- **A calendar or address book that stops answering says so without a restart.** A calendar or address book that stops answering now says so after two refreshes, and takes no changes until it answers again.
- **A quoted message is dated when it was sent.** A reply or forward now gives the date the original message was sent, rather than the date it reached your mailbox.
- **A signature's and a quote's plain text follow what their HTML shows.** The plain-text version of a signature or quote from a draft written in another app no longer picks up stray line breaks and spaces.
- **An account's page no longer holds up the window while an account reconnects.** Settings no longer stops responding while an account reconnects.
- **Changing a subscription shows that it is working.** Changing your subscription in Settings now shows that it is working, and the subscription is brought up to date when you return to the app.
- **Signing an account in again through the browser works the same everywhere.** Signing an account in again through your browser now shows that it is working, can be cancelled, and can be started again if you left the browser.
- **Syncing accounts with your other devices is run by the core.** Changing how an account is synced with your other devices now shows that it is working and says when it didn’t take.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **Choose the default calendar on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Windows.** Choose which calendar a new event goes in, under Settings → Calendar.
- **The desktop formatting toolbar keeps to one row.** The formatting toolbar keeps to one row, with anything that does not fit behind its ⋯ button, and the message no longer sits in a box of its own.
- **The formatting toolbar stays in view while writing a long message.** The formatting toolbar stays in view while you write a long message, and the line you are typing is never cut off at the bottom of the window.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A tall invitation card scrolls inside the Mac reading pane.** On the Mac, a long meeting invitation now scrolls inside the reading pane, so Accept, Maybe and Decline stay within reach.
- **An account's page keeps its place when a notice appears above it.** An account's page in Settings → Accounts no longer jumps when a notice appears at its top while you are scrolled further down.
- **Discarding a message in a window of its own closes the window.** Discarding a message in a window of its own now closes the window, and the draft no longer returns to Drafts.
- **Mail that arrives as the app opens gets its notification.** A message that arrives in the moment the app opens now shows a notification, like any other new mail.
- **Setup no longer flashes its first screen as it closes.** Finishing account setup no longer shows the "Add your account" screen for a moment as the window closes.
- **The Mac window holds still whatever a message holds.** On the Mac, a long meeting invitation no longer moves the whole window's contents, so the action bar and Settings stay in view, and the reading pane is no longer cut off in a narrow window.
