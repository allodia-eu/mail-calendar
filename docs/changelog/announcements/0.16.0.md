# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.16.0

Released 6 November 2026.

This release brings video meetings to every app, and makes adding your Nextcloud calendar a lot easier! 🚀

- **Add a video meeting to an event**: as you create or edit an event, add a Microsoft Teams or Google Meet meeting on their own calendars, a Jitsi Meet meeting on your own server (set it in Settings), or a Teams or Nextcloud Talk meeting through your Microsoft work account or Nextcloud account, on any calendar. Available on every app.
- **Join video meetings on every app**: joining an event's video meeting from its detail now works on Windows, Android and Linux too, with its meeting ID, passcode and dial-in numbers beside it.
- **Sign in with Nextcloud**: add the calendar and address book on your Nextcloud server by signing in through Nextcloud in your browser, single sign-on included, with no app password to make yourself. Available on every app.
- **Easier JMAP setup**: when your mail server has no calendar or address book of its own but one was found beside it, setup now offers to add it, on macOS, iPhone, iPad and Windows. On macOS, iPhone and iPad, setup also asks which of mail, calendar and contacts you want to use.

**The latest version is available through our website: [https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar](https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar)**

**Store availability**: ⏳ this version is in review in the stores; this line changes to ✅ once it is live on all supported platforms.

The changes below are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **Add a Teams or Talk meeting through a connected account.** Add a Microsoft Teams or Nextcloud Talk meeting to an event on any calendar, through your Microsoft work account or your Nextcloud account.
- **Add a video meeting to an event.** Add a video meeting to an event as you create or edit it: Teams or Google Meet on their own calendars, or Jitsi Meet on your own server, set in Settings.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A Teams or Talk choice is offered as soon as its account connects.** Teams and Talk meetings are now offered in the event editor as soon as their account connects.
- **A use switched during a reconnect stays switched.** Switching an account’s calendar or contacts on or off while it reconnects now takes effect straight away.
- **Long notes and a Teams meeting survive an edit of a Microsoft event.** Editing the notes of an event on a Microsoft calendar no longer cuts long notes short or removes its Teams meeting.
- **Say when an Allodia account sign-in has ended.** When your Allodia account sign-in has ended, the app now says so in a banner and offers to sign in again, instead of saying in Settings that your subscription could not be checked.
- **Signing in again to an account set up under another spelling of its address.** Signing in again to an email account set up from this version on under an alias of its address no longer says you signed in to a different account.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **Sign in with Nextcloud on macOS, iPhone and iPad.** A calendar and address book on a Nextcloud server can now be added by signing in through Nextcloud in your browser, single sign-on included, without making an app password yourself.
- **The calendar found beside a JMAP server, offered after setup on macOS, iPhone and iPad.** When your mail server has no calendar or address book of its own but one was found beside it, setup now offers to add it as well.
- **What a JMAP account is used for, asked after its sign-in on macOS, iPhone and iPad.** After you sign in to a JMAP account, setup now asks which of mail, calendar and contacts on its server you want to use.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A dial-in number with its PIN reads on a CalDAV calendar.** A meeting's dial-in number with its PIN attached is no longer left out on some CalDAV calendars.
- **A meeting's numbers pass the link policy.** A number in an event's video meeting that would run a phone service code is no longer offered to dial, and a dial-in number with its PIN attached dials the number alone and shows the PIN to type.
- **The 12/24-hour clock reaches mail.** Times in the message list, the message header and the quoted text of a reply now follow the 12/24-hour setting, as the calendar already did.
- **The calendar card the link step opens says what it adds.** Setting up the calendar found beside your mail account now says it adds to that account, and mentions that some servers need an app password.
- **The invitation card's answer buttons take a click across their whole face.** Accept, Maybe and Decline on a meeting invitation now respond wherever you click them, not only on part of the button.
