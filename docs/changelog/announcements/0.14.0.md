# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.14.0

Released 27 October 2026.

This release carries 20 changes, and most of them are about syncing: it is more dependable across providers, and on several of them quicker. Measured on our own test accounts: adding a Yahoo account with about 1,500 messages in its inbox, the first messages now appear after about 3 seconds rather than 22 to 24, and the rest follow as they download instead of all at once at the end. The first sync of an iCloud calendar holding 6,114 events takes about 14 seconds rather than 55. And a Gmail account with a lot of mail, which Google's usage limit used to stop on every attempt so that it never finished, now downloads at a pace Google accepts (855 messages in three and a half minutes, without a refusal) and carries on where it stopped. Beside those: every calendar of an account now syncs, not only the first; iCloud and Yahoo calendars and address books sync; and the app icon shows how many messages are unread on macOS, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Linux.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **A calendar or address book server that moves is followed, and one on another site is asked about first.** When your calendar or address book server moves, the app follows it, and if the new server is run by someone else it asks you first instead of sending your password there.
- **Every calendar of an account syncs, and takes new events.** Every calendar of an account now syncs and can take new events, not only the first, and a refresh with nothing new is quicker.
- **iCloud and Yahoo calendars and address books sync, and Fastmail address books appear.** iCloud and Yahoo calendars and address books now sync, and Fastmail address books now appear.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A large Gmail mailbox finishes downloading, and carries on where it stopped.** A Gmail account with a lot of mail now finishes downloading, continues where it stopped, and keeps receiving new mail meanwhile.
- **A new IMAP account's mail appears as it downloads.** When you add an IMAP account, its messages now appear as they download instead of all at once at the end.
- **A new account's list no longer says it is empty while it downloads.** While a new account's mail is downloading, the list no longer says there are no messages.
- **A pushed message waits for a busy folder.** A message that arrived while another sync was running no longer stays hidden until the next check.
- **Contacts save on Fastmail.** Saving a contact on a Fastmail account works again, and its name keeps the order you typed it in.
- **Every calendar has a colour of its own.** Each calendar now has a colour of its own, across all your accounts, including iCloud and Yahoo calendars that all showed the same colour.
- **Gmail and Microsoft mail appears as it downloads, and stays when a sync stops partway.** Gmail and Microsoft accounts now show messages as they download, and keep them if a sync is stopped partway.
- **Google contacts keep updating after a week without a sync.** Google contacts no longer stop updating when the app has gone a week or more without syncing them.
- **Invitations can be answered on Microsoft, Google and OAuth JMAP calendars.** Accepting, declining or answering "maybe" to a meeting now works on Microsoft, Google and other OAuth calendar accounts.
- **Retrying a calendar or address book leaves the mailbox alone.** When a calendar or address book could not be reached, trying again no longer signs in to the mailbox again, so a provider such as Yahoo no longer limits the account's sign-ins.
- **Setup finds an iCloud calendar.** Adding an iCloud account now offers its calendar as well as its address book.

## 🪟 Windows

### ✨ New

- **Unread count on the app icon.** The app icon now shows how many messages in your inboxes are unread, the same number as the Inbox in the sidebar.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **The unified Inbox count follows the account Inboxes on Windows.** The unread count on All Accounts › Inbox now changes as soon as the count on an account’s Inbox does.

---

## 🐧 Linux

### ✨ New

- **Unread count in the dock on Linux.** Docks that show counts on their icons, such as Ubuntu Dock and KDE Plasma's, now show how many messages in your inboxes are unread.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A message's attachments can be reached with the keyboard.** The attachments of an open message, and their Open and Save buttons, can now be reached with the keyboard.
- **An error shown as Settings opens is drawn above it.** An error shown as Settings opens now appears in front of it rather than behind it on desktops such as Sway.
- **The calendar no longer redraws itself when nothing in it has changed.** Clicking an event in the calendar now always opens it, and the calendar keeps your keyboard place.
