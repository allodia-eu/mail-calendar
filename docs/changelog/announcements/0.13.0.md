# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.13.0

Released 19 October 2026.

This release carries 48 changes, and three of them are what it is about. Meetings can now be handled from the calendar on every app: accept, decline or answer maybe to one from its event and change the answer later, invite people to an event as required or optional, and change who is invited to a meeting you organise. In a dark app, messages are now shown dark as well, with their colours adjusted to suit and a sun and moon button above each message to switch back. And undo and redo now work while writing on every app, in the message and in each address field, where a removed recipient comes back whole. Beside those: a mail account can switch between a password and signing in with its provider, a calendar on a separate server can sign in through its own provider, and when calendar or contacts do not connect, Settings, the calendar and contacts say so and why.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **Answer a meeting from the calendar.** Accept, answer maybe to or decline a meeting from its event in the calendar, and change your answer later. The organiser is told as when you answer the invitation email.
- **Calendar and contacts say when they are not connected.** When an account’s calendar or address book isn’t connected, the calendar and contacts now say so and show what was last synced, and a change that wasn’t saved no longer reads as saved.
- **Invitations through a linked calendar.** An invitation in a mailbox linked to a calendar account is now put on, and answered from, that calendar, with any reply emailed from the mailbox.
- **Invite people to an event.** Invite people to a new event, as required or optional, and change who is invited to a meeting you organise. The calendar's server sends the invitations.
- **Keep a calendar the sign-in does not reach.** When signing in through your provider would cost your calendar and contacts, the app asks first and can keep them on your password as a separate, linked account.
- **Messages follow a dark app.** In a dark app, messages are now shown on a dark background with their colours adjusted to suit; the sun and moon button above a message switches every message back to its light background, and the same switch is in Settings → Reading.
- **Settings says when calendar or contacts did not connect.** When your calendar or contacts can’t connect, the account’s page in Settings now says why, and a server that couldn’t be reached is tried again when you refresh.
- **Sign in to a calendar server of its own.** A calendar on a separate server can now sign in through that server's own provider, where it offers one, instead of a password.
- **Switch how an account signs in.** A mail account can now switch between a password and signing in with your provider in Settings, keeping its mail and settings.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A font size picked with nothing selected.** Choosing a font size with nothing selected now applies that size to what you type next, rather than a much larger one.
- **A separate calendar server says what did not connect, and a slow one is given time.** Setting up a calendar or address book on a server of its own now waits longer for a slow server and says which one did not connect, and a sign-in the browser returned from an earlier attempt now says to sign in again.
- **Adding an account that is already set up.** Adding an account that is already set up now says so and leaves the existing account as it is, where it used to replace it and lose its calendar and contacts.
- **An IMAP sign-in keeps its renewal.** An email account connected by signing in with your provider no longer asks you to sign in again after the app restarts.
- **Answer routes follow the calendar server's address set.** An answer to an invitation sent to an address your calendar server does not handle is now emailed to the organiser rather than only saved.
- **No false offline after a network change.** The app no longer says it is offline on a working connection after the network changed for a moment.
- **Opening a folder while mail is updating.** Opening a folder while your mail is updating no longer sends you back to the folder you just left.
- **Pasting lists from Word, merged cells and pictures.** Lists pasted from Word or Outlook stay lists with no blank line after them, merged table cells no longer shift the rows below, and a picture copied with text inside a message moves with it.
- **Return or leaving the field finishes an address.** An address typed into To, Cc or Bcc now becomes its own recipient when you press Return or move to another field, not only after a comma.
- **Sender photos no longer flash to initials.** Sender photos in the message list no longer flash to initials and back when a draft is saved or contacts sync.
- **Sign in at providers on an earlier draft of the standard.** Signing in with your provider now works at providers that follow an earlier version of the sign-in standard, such as Soverin, and the button is no longer offered where signing in cannot work.
- **Tell apart accounts with one address.** Settings tells apart two accounts with the same address by naming their kind or server.
- **The calendar fills in sooner at launch.** After the app starts, your calendar now fills in without waiting for your mail to finish downloading.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **Keyboard commands on the Mac, iPad and iPhone.** New Mail, New Event, the calendar views, Back to Today, moving through the calendar, Manage Calendars and Sync are now in the menu bar, with keyboard shortcuts that also work on iPad and iPhone with a keyboard.
- **Selecting messages the way the platform does on Mac, iPhone and iPad.** On Mac, ⌘A and Edit ▸ Select All now select every message in the list, and the arrow keys move through it. On iPhone and iPad, Select shows checkmarks and ⌘A on a keyboard selects every message.
- **The calendar's controls in the toolbar on Mac, iPhone and iPad.** The calendar's buttons now sit in the toolbar, where they are larger and easier to reach, and on iPhone and iPad the month view turns with a swipe.
- **Undo and redo while writing, on a Mac, iPhone and iPad.** Undo and redo now work while writing: ⌘Z, the Edit menu, a shake or the iPad's shortcut bar takes back the newest change in the message or the field you are in, ⇧⌘Z puts it back, and a removed recipient comes back whole.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A notification that starts the app opens its message.** A new-mail notification now opens its message even when the app was closed.
- **Add an account from Settings on a Mac, iPhone and iPad.** Another account is now added from Settings → Accounts, where Add account sits above the accounts already set up, rather than from the bottom of the folder list.
- **Two accounts' new mail no longer shares one notification.** New mail in two accounts no longer replaces one account's notification with the other's.

## 🪟 Windows

### ✨ New

- **Click a new-mail notification to open that message.** Clicking a new-mail notification now opens that message instead of just bringing the app forward.
- **Undo and redo while writing.** Undo and redo now work while writing: Ctrl+Z takes back the newest change in the message or the field you are in, Shift+Ctrl+Z or Ctrl+Y puts it back, and a removed recipient comes back whole.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **Closed windows free their memory on Windows.** Message and draft windows give their memory back when you close them.
- **Copies of forwarded and reopened files are deleted on Windows.** Copies of attachments made for forwards and reopened drafts no longer pile up on disk.
- **File dialogs on Windows.** Save and open dialogs appear over the window you opened them from, and a save that fails no longer removes the file you chose to replace.
- **No web inspector on Windows.** Messages and drafts can no longer be opened in the browser's developer tools, by you or by another program on the computer.
- **The Windows found card names its servers.** When adding an account, the servers we found are now listed for you to confirm instead of shown as fields to fill in.
- **The email keyboard in address fields.** Address fields, such as To and the invitee field, now bring up the email keyboard with @ at hand, and no longer correct what you type.
