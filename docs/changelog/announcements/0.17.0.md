# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.17.0

Released 9 November 2026.

This release brings calmer notices, colour to the calendar and one command bar for your mail! 🚀

- **Notices that wait for you**: anything that needs your attention now shows in one place per screen, with its own button, instead of interrupting. That includes questions about a missing Sent copy, an undelivered invitation reply or a new time zone, and each account's page in Settings says whether its undelivered replies are emailed. Available on every app.
- **A calendar in colour**: events are tinted in their calendar's colour, show long titles in full where there is room, and mark repeating events. On macOS, iPhone and iPad the calendar also scrolls and zooms smoothly. Available on every app.
- **One command bar for your mail**: on macOS, Windows and Linux, New Mail and the mail actions share one bar that fits any window width and includes Move. On every app, Delete always moves mail to Trash first, deleting for good only from Trash after asking.
- **Write in a window of its own**: a message you are writing beside the mailbox can be moved into its own window, with everything you have written so far. Available on macOS, Windows and Linux.

**The latest version is available through our website: [https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar](https://allodia.eu/mail-calendar)**

**Store availability**: ⏳ this version is in review in the stores; this line changes to ✅ once it is live on all supported platforms.

The changes below are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **One command bar across the mail window, and Delete always goes through Trash.** New Mail and the mail actions now share one bar that fits any window width and includes Move, and Delete always moves mail to Trash first, deleting for good only from Trash after asking.
- **The three questions are notices, and replies are chosen per account in Settings.** Questions about a missing Sent copy, an undelivered invitation reply or a new time zone now wait as notices instead of interrupting, and each account’s page in Settings says whether its undelivered replies are emailed.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A Try again that does not get through says so.** When Try again on a notice still cannot reach the server, the notice now says so.
- **A calendar can be written as soon as its account reconnects.** A calendar can now be written to as soon as its account reconnects, without waiting for a sync.
- **A calendar on a server of its own is signed in to from the core's form.** The form for a calendar or address book on a server of its own now shows that Connect or Skip is working until it is done, on every platform.
- **A flat formatting toolbar that follows a dark appearance.** The formatting toolbar above a message you are writing is plainer, with icons for lists, and turns dark in a dark appearance; the message itself stays white, as its recipient will see it.
- **An account's Sign-in section follows the page's other controls.** In Settings, an account's Sign-in section is where a refused password is entered again, and it shows that a new password is being tried until the server answers.
- **An account's name and mail settings follow the page's other controls.** In Settings, an account's name now sits at the top of its page, and its mail settings show that a change is working until it is done.
- **An account's page shows a change running until it is done.** Switching what an account is used for, linking it or removing it in Settings now shows that it is working until it is done.
- **An account's servers follow the page's other controls.** In Settings, saving an account's servers now shows that it is working, says which field still needs filling in, and keeps what you typed if the servers are refused.
- **Saving a sender name no longer pauses the app.** Saving the name your mail is sent under no longer pauses the app while the server answers.
- **Shared mailboxes follow the page's other controls.** In Settings, switching a shared mailbox or adding one now shows that it is working, and an address that wasn't found stays to be corrected.
- **Signing in again to grant a permission works at once.** After you sign in again to give a missing permission, your calendar now connects straight away instead of at the next launch.
- **The Allodia sign-in notice turns urgent a week before a paid feature stops.** If your Allodia sign-in has ended and a paid feature will stop within a week, the notice now says so in the warning colour.
