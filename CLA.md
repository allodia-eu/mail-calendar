# Contributor License Agreement

Before your first contribution is merged you will be asked to agree to this. It is short, and this
first section is the part that actually matters.

## Why this exists

The app is GPL-3.0-only, and Allodia also publishes it on the Apple App Store, whose terms impose
restrictions the GPL does not permit a distributor to add. Allodia's own store builds therefore
ship under a different, proprietary licence: this is only possible if Allodia holds a licence to
**every** line in them, including yours.

That is the whole purpose. It is not a copyright assignment: you keep your copyright, and you keep
every right to your own work that you had before.

**Allodia's side of it** is [`docs/pledge.md`](docs/pledge.md), and in particular that this
repository stays free and open source, that no feature that is free today is ever moved behind a
payment, and that a fork needs nothing from Allodia. That document is written to be quotable
against us.

Once, on your first pull request. A bot posts the link and records your agreement against your
GitHub account; later pull requests need nothing further. If you would rather not agree, open an
issue describing the change instead: a good bug report is a real contribution, and this agreement
does not apply to one.
